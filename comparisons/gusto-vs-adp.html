Gusto vs ADP (2026): Transparent Pricing vs Enterprise Power — Which Is Right for You?

Gusto and ADP represent two fundamentally different approaches to payroll. Gusto is the modern, transparent-pricing platform built specifically for small businesses. ADP is the 75-year-old industry giant that processes payroll for 1 in 6 American workers. We've compared both across pricing, payroll, HR, benefits, integrations, and more — with real 2026 data, not recycled 2022 content.

⚡ Quick Verdict

🟢 Choose Gusto If…

You're a small-to-midsize business (1-100 employees) that values transparent pricing, modern UX, and simplicity. Gusto starts at $49/month + $6/employee with everything included — payroll, tax filing, benefits administration, onboarding, and compliance. No hidden fees, no year-end surprises, no sales calls required. If you want payroll that just works without the enterprise overhead, Gusto is the clear choice.

🔵 Choose ADP If…

You're a growing or mid-to-large business (50-1,000+ employees) that needs maximum scalability, global payroll, or deep integrations. ADP processes payroll in 140+ countries, offers 500+ third-party integrations through ADP Marketplace, and provides workforce analytics powered by the largest payroll dataset in the world. 💡 The honest truth: For 80% of small businesses reading this comparison, Gusto is the better choice. ADP's real advantages — global payroll, DataCloud analytics, enterprise talent management — are capabilities most businesses under 100 employees don't need. You'll pay more for ADP and use less of it. The exception: if you know you'll scale past 100 employees within 2-3 years, starting with ADP avoids a platform migration later.

Overview: Two Different Philosophies

The Gusto vs ADP comparison isn't just about features — it's about two fundamentally different approaches to building a payroll company.

Gusto was founded in 2011 (originally as ZenPayroll) with a simple thesis: payroll and HR software for small businesses shouldn't require a sales call, a contract, or a PhD to operate. Gusto publishes its pricing on its website, offers month-to-month billing, includes benefits administration at no extra charge, and designs its interface for business owners who aren't HR professionals. As of 2026, Gusto serves over 300,000 businesses and processes payroll for more than 6 million employees nationwide.

ADP was founded in 1949 — six decades before Gusto existed. It's the largest payroll company in the world, publicly traded (NASDAQ: ADP) with over $19 billion in annual revenue, 1 million+ clients, and operations in 140+ countries. ADP processes payroll for an estimated 1 in 6 American workers. Its approach is enterprise-first: modular products, custom pricing, sales-driven onboarding, and capabilities that scale from a 5-person startup to a 50,000-employee multinational.

This fundamental difference shapes everything — pricing, user experience, support, and the trade-offs you'll make as a customer. Gusto optimizes for the small business owner who wants simplicity and predictability. ADP optimizes for the organization that needs power and scalability. Understanding which philosophy fits your business is the key to choosing correctly.

In 2026, the gap between these platforms has both narrowed and widened. Gusto has added more advanced HR features, expanded to all 50 states for benefits brokerage, and launched Gusto Embedded (payroll APIs for other platforms). ADP has modernized its small business product (ADP Run) and invested in AI-powered analytics. But the core difference remains: Gusto charges you what it says it will; ADP charges you what it negotiates with you — and those two approaches create very different customer experiences.

What Is Gusto?

Gusto is a cloud-based payroll, benefits, and HR platform headquartered in San Francisco and Denver. Founded in 2011 by Josh Reeves, Edward Kim, and Tomer London, Gusto has raised over $746 million in venture funding and was valued at approximately $9.5 billion as of its last funding round. The company serves over 300,000 businesses across all 50 U.S. states, with a focus on companies with 1-200 employees.

Gusto Plans (2026 Pricing)

Gusto is one of the few payroll providers that publishes pricing on its website — a deliberate philosophical choice that reflects its commitment to transparency:

  • Simple — $49/month + $6/employee/month
    Full-service payroll, tax filing in all 50 states, employee self-service, basic hiring and onboarding tools, health insurance administration, employee financial benefits, Gusto-brokered health insurance, and integrations. Designed for straightforward payroll needs.
  • Plus — $80/month + $12/employee/month
    Everything in Simple plus: full HR tools (employee directory, org chart, custom permissions), time tracking and PTO management, project tracking, workforce costing, hiring and applicant tracking, performance reviews, employee surveys, and advanced onboarding with custom checklists. The most popular plan for growing businesses.
  • Premium — Custom pricing (25+ employees)
    Everything in Plus, plus: dedicated customer success manager, HR resource center with compliance alerts, access to certified HR experts, custom admin permissions, priority support, and migration assistance. Designed for established businesses with more complex HR needs.
  • Contractor-only — $35/month + $6/contractor/month
    Pay contractors (W-2 not included), automatic Form 1099 filing, contractor self-service portal, and unlimited payments. Ideal for businesses that only work with independent contractors.

Gusto Core Capabilities

  • Full-service payroll: Automated payroll with next-day or 2-day direct deposit. Run payroll in minutes with AutoPilot (fully automatic payroll for salaried employees). Handles hourly, salaried, and contract workers. Multiple pay schedules supported. Unlimited payroll runs at no extra charge — including off-cycle, bonus, and correction runs.
  • Tax filing: Gusto calculates, files, and remits all federal, state, and local payroll taxes automatically. This includes quarterly (Form 941) and annual filings (Form 940, W-2, W-3, 1099). Gusto also handles state tax registration for new states at no additional charge — when you hire an employee in a new state, Gusto registers you automatically. This is a significant advantage over ADP, which often charges for this.
  • Benefits administration: Gusto is a licensed insurance broker in all 50 states. Shop health, dental, vision, life, and disability insurance from multiple carriers directly within the platform. Benefits enrollment integrates with payroll for automatic deductions. Also offers HSAs, FSAs, commuter benefits, 529 college savings, and workers' compensation (pay-as-you-go).
  • 401(k) retirement: Partnership with Guideline for affordable 401(k) plans. Plan administration, compliance testing, and employee self-service included. Guideline charges $49/month + $8/participant — significantly more affordable than traditional 401(k) providers.
  • Hiring and onboarding: Job posting distribution, offer letter templates, digital document signing, custom onboarding checklists, I-9 and W-4 collection, and E-Verify integration. New employees complete paperwork before their first day.
  • Gusto Wallet: A financial wellness app for employees that provides early wage access (Gusto Cashout — up to $1,000 between paychecks), fee-free savings accounts, budgeting tools, and spending accounts. No fees to employees or employers. This is a unique benefit that helps with employee retention.
  • Time tracking (Plus/Premium): Built-in time tracking with GPS geolocation, mobile clock-in, project tracking, PTO management, and overtime calculations. Data flows directly to payroll. No add-on fee — included in Plus and Premium plans.
  • HR tools (Plus/Premium): Employee directory, org chart, custom reporting, performance reviews, employee surveys, document storage, and compliance alerts. The Premium plan adds access to certified HR experts for employment law questions.
  • Contractor payments: Pay contractors via direct deposit or check. Automatic Form 1099 generation and filing at year-end. Contractor self-service portal for invoice submission and tax document access. International contractor payments available in 80+ countries.
  • Gusto Embedded: A payroll API that allows other software platforms to embed Gusto's payroll engine into their products. Used by accounting firms, vertical SaaS platforms, and fintech companies to offer payroll without building it from scratch.

Who Uses Gusto?

  • Small businesses (1-50 employees) — Gusto's sweet spot. Simple, predictable pricing with everything included.
  • Startups — No contracts, month-to-month billing, transparent pricing. Easy to start and easy to switch if needed.
  • Businesses with mixed W-2 and 1099 workers — Gusto handles both employee payroll and contractor payments in one platform.
  • Remote/distributed teams — Multi-state tax registration included free. Hire in new states without paperwork or extra fees.
  • Accounting firms — Gusto for accountants offers a dedicated dashboard for firms managing payroll for multiple clients.
  • Growing businesses (50-200 employees) — Plus and Premium plans scale with more advanced HR, performance management, and compliance tools.

What Is ADP?

ADP (Automatic Data Processing) is the world's largest payroll and human capital management (HCM) provider, founded in 1949 in Paterson, New Jersey. ADP is publicly traded (NASDAQ: ADP) with over $19 billion in annual revenue, approximately 64,000 employees, and operations in 140+ countries. ADP serves more than 1 million clients worldwide and pays an estimated 1 in 6 U.S. workers.

ADP Product Portfolio

ADP offers multiple products segmented by business size — which is both its strength (purpose-built for your scale) and its complexity (many products to navigate):

  • ADP Run Powered by ADP (1-49 employees) — The small business platform with four tiers: Essential Payroll, Enhanced Payroll, Complete Payroll & HR, and HR Pro. Handles payroll, tax filing, new hire reporting, and basic-to-advanced HR depending on tier. The Complete and HR Pro tiers add ZipRecruiter job posting, employee handbook builder, proactive compliance alerts, and employee assistance programs.
  • ADP Workforce Now (50-1,000+ employees) — The mid-market flagship platform. Modular design: Payroll, HR Management, Talent (recruitment, performance, compensation), Benefits, Time & Attendance, and Analytics. ADP's most popular product and strongest offering.
  • ADP Vantage HCM (1,000+ employees) — Enterprise HCM suite with global payroll, comprehensive talent management, advanced analytics, and strategic workforce planning.
  • ADP TotalSource (PEO, 5-250+ employees) — Professional Employer Organization providing co-employment with Fortune 500-level benefits, dedicated HR business partners, compliance management, and workers' comp administration.
  • ADP GlobalView & Celergo — International payroll solutions for multinationals, covering 140+ countries through direct presence and local payroll partners.

ADP Core Capabilities

  • Payroll processing: Industry-leading payroll engine handling every scenario: hourly, salaried, commission, tipped, multi-state, multi-country, garnishments, retroactive adjustments, and complex compensation structures. Same-day direct deposit available. Voice-activated payroll through Alexa/Google Assistant (ADP Run).
  • Tax services: Comprehensive tax compliance across all 50 states, territories, and 140+ countries. Federal, state, local, and international tax filing. Year-end processing (W-2, 1099, ACA forms). Tax penalty guarantee.
  • ADP Marketplace: 500+ pre-built integrations with certified partners covering accounting, benefits, time tracking, learning, talent, ERP, and more. The largest payroll integration ecosystem available.
  • ADP DataCloud: People analytics powered by anonymized data from ADP's enormous client base. Benchmark compensation, turnover, diversity, and workforce trends against industry and geographic peers. No competitor has this dataset.
  • Talent management (Workforce Now): Recruitment with ZipRecruiter integration, applicant tracking, onboarding, performance management, compensation planning, and succession planning.
  • Wisely by ADP: Pay card and financial wellness platform for employees — fee-free ATM withdrawals, budgeting tools, and instant wage access.

Who Uses ADP?

  • Mid-market businesses (50-1,000 employees) — ADP Workforce Now is the industry standard for this segment.
  • Enterprise organizations (1,000+ employees) — ADP Vantage HCM and GlobalView serve Fortune 500 companies.
  • Businesses with global employees — 140+ country payroll coverage is unmatched.
  • Data-driven HR teams — ADP DataCloud's benchmarking is unique in the industry.
  • High-growth companies — Start with ADP Run, scale to Workforce Now without migrating platforms.
  • Small businesses (1-49) — ADP Run serves this segment, though Gusto often provides a better small-business experience.

Pricing Compared: Transparency vs Opacity

Pricing is the single biggest difference between Gusto and ADP — and it's not just about the dollar amounts. It's about the entire purchasing experience.

Gusto Pricing (Published, Transparent)

  • Simple — $49/month + $6/employee/month
    Full-service payroll, tax filing, benefits admin, basic onboarding, employee self-service, Gusto Wallet, integrations. W-2/1099 filing included. Unlimited payroll runs.
  • Plus — $80/month + $12/employee/month
    Everything in Simple + time tracking, PTO management, project tracking, hiring/ATS, performance reviews, employee surveys, workforce costing, advanced onboarding, custom permissions.
  • Premium — Custom pricing (25+ employees)
    Everything in Plus + dedicated CSM, HR resource center, certified HR experts, priority support, compliance alerts, custom admin roles.
  • Contractor-only — $35/month + $6/contractor/month
    Contractor payments, automatic 1099 filing, contractor self-service. No W-2 payroll.

✅ No setup fees. No year-end fees. No extra charge for state tax registration. No contract — cancel anytime. What you see is what you pay.

ADP Pricing (Unpublished, Quote-Based)

  • ADP Run Essential — Estimated $59-$79/month base + $4-$8/employee. Basic payroll and tax filing.
  • ADP Run Enhanced — Estimated $79-$120/month base + $6-$10/employee. Adds background checks, SUI management, ZipRecruiter.
  • ADP Run Complete Payroll & HR — Estimated $120-$160+/month base + $8-$12/employee. Adds HR Help Desk, handbook, compliance alerts.
  • ADP Run HR Pro — Estimated $160-$200+/month base + $10-$16/employee. Full HR suite with EAP.
  • ADP Workforce Now — Estimated $10-$30+/employee/month (modular). Annual contracts standard.

⚠️ ADP does not publish pricing. These are estimates from industry reports and user data. Your actual quote will vary based on negotiation, location, and services selected. Common add-on charges include: W-2/1099 processing, state tax registration, setup fees, off-cycle payroll runs, and year-end processing.

The Real Cost Difference

On paper, ADP Run Essential appears slightly cheaper per employee than Gusto Simple. But the real cost includes what's not in that base price:

  • Year-end processing: Gusto includes W-2 and 1099 filing. ADP may charge $50-$200+ for year-end forms.
  • State tax registration: Gusto registers you in new states free. ADP often charges per state registration.
  • Off-cycle payroll: Gusto offers unlimited runs. ADP may charge for additional payroll runs beyond your standard schedule.
  • Setup/implementation: Gusto has no setup fee. ADP may charge $50-$200+ for implementation.
  • Price increases: Gusto adjusts pricing transparently and occasionally (you'll see it on the website). ADP's annual contract renewals frequently include 5-15% increases that users report as unexpected.

Bottom line: When you factor in hidden fees, ADP typically costs 15-30% more than Gusto for a comparable small business setup. And Gusto's all-inclusive pricing means you'll never get an unexpected charge on your invoice.

Payroll Processing & Tax Filing

Both platforms excel at the fundamentals — but the experience of running payroll feels very different.

Gusto Payroll

  • AutoPilot payroll: Gusto's signature feature. For salaried employees with consistent pay, set it once and payroll runs automatically on schedule with no manual intervention. You get a notification before each run and can cancel or adjust if needed. This alone saves hours per month.
  • Unlimited payroll runs: Run payroll as many times as you need — regular runs, off-cycle payments, bonuses, commissions, corrections — all at no extra charge. ADP may charge for additional runs.
  • Flexible pay types: Hourly, salaried, commission, bonus, tips, overtime, shift differentials, and retroactive pay adjustments.
  • Direct deposit options: 2-day direct deposit standard, next-day available on Plus and Premium. Gusto Cashout gives employees early access to earned wages between paydays.
  • Tax filing: Gusto calculates, files, and pays all federal, state, and local payroll taxes. Quarterly (941) and annual (940, W-2, 1099) filings included. State tax registration in new states handled automatically and free.
  • Garnishments: Child support, tax levies, and creditor garnishments processed and remitted with proper priority handling.
  • Payroll reports: Payroll journal, tax liability, paycheck details, department/location summaries, custom reports on Plus/Premium.
  • Accounting integration: One-click sync with QuickBooks Online, Xero, and FreshBooks. Custom journal entry mapping available.

ADP Payroll

  • Processing options: Online, mobile, or voice-activated (Alexa/Google Assistant on ADP Run). Manager approval workflows for payroll review before processing.
  • Pay types: Everything Gusto handles plus multi-country payroll, piece-rate, draws, and extremely complex compensation structures. ADP's payroll engine handles edge cases that Gusto won't encounter.
  • Direct deposit: Same-day and next-day options available. Paper checks and Wisely pay cards (wage access, budgeting, fee-free ATM) for employees without bank accounts.
  • Tax filing: The most comprehensive tax engine in the industry. All 50 states, territories, and 140+ countries. Federal, state, local, and international jurisdictions. ACA reporting (1095-B/C). Tax penalty guarantee.
  • Garnishments: Full garnishment management with multi-state compliance, creditor correspondence, and priority-based processing across complex scenarios.
  • ADP DataCloud payroll analytics: Benchmark your payroll costs, overtime trends, and compensation data against ADP's massive anonymous dataset.
  • General ledger integration: Native integrations with QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, NetSuite through ADP Marketplace.

🏆 Verdict: Payroll Processing

For small business payroll (1-100 employees), Gusto wins on experience. AutoPilot payroll, unlimited runs, free state registration, and a cleaner interface make running payroll genuinely effortless. The time savings from AutoPilot alone can be significant — you're not clicking "approve" every two weeks for salaried employees.

For complex or international payroll, ADP wins on power. Multi-country payroll, advanced garnishment handling, DataCloud analytics, and the deepest tax engine in the industry make ADP the right choice for businesses with complex payroll needs. If you're processing payroll in multiple countries, Gusto isn't an option — ADP (or Deel/Rippling) is.

ADP HR

  • Onboarding: Customizable onboarding workflows with task assignment, welcome messages, and paperless new hire processing. Seamless transition from recruitment (if using ADP's ATS).
  • Employee self-service (MyADP): One of the most downloaded HR apps globally. Employees manage pay stubs, benefits, PTO, and personal information.

  • Employee handbook builder (Complete/HR Pro): Create compliant handbooks with state-specific policies.

🏆 Verdict: HR

ADP wins for HR depth and enterprise capabilities — talent acquisition, succession planning, compensation management, and DataCloud analytics are leagues ahead of what Gusto offers. If you have a dedicated HR team that needs these tools, ADP Workforce Now is the right choice.

Gusto wins for HR that small businesses actually use. Performance reviews, onboarding, surveys, and compliance alerts cover 90% of what a 10-50 employee business needs. Gusto's advantage is that these features are included on Plus — not sold as separate modules at additional cost. For a small business without a dedicated HR department, Gusto gives you what you need without what you don't.

Gusto Benefits

🏆 Verdict: Benefits

Gusto wins for small businesses. The fact that benefits administration is included at no extra charge on every plan — including insurance brokerage in all 50 states — is remarkable. For a 20-person business setting up benefits for the first time, Gusto makes the process straightforward: shop plans, employees enroll online, deductions sync with payroll automatically. Gusto Wallet's financial wellness features (earned wage access, savings accounts) are also a genuine employee retention tool that ADP doesn't match at the same level for small businesses.

ADP wins for complex benefits environments — businesses with intricate eligibility rules, carrier EDI feeds, voluntary benefit packages, and large workforces. ADP TotalSource's PEO benefits (Fortune 500-level plans for small businesses) are also compelling if you're willing to enter a co-employment arrangement.

Compliance & Tax Compliance

Gusto Compliance

  • Tax accuracy guarantee: Gusto covers penalties and interest from tax filing errors they cause.
  • New hire reporting: Automated state new hire reporting for all 50 states.
  • I-9 and E-Verify: Electronic I-9 completion with optional E-Verify integration.
  • ACA compliance: Eligibility tracking and 1095-C form generation (for applicable large employers).
  • Compliance alerts (Premium): Proactive notifications about employment law changes. Access to certified HR professionals for compliance questions.
  • Workers' comp: Pay-as-you-go workers' compensation insurance with real-time payroll data integration.
  • Document storage: Secure storage for I-9s, tax forms, and compliance documentation.

ADP Compliance

  • Multi-state and international tax: The most comprehensive tax compliance system available — all 50 states, territories, and 140+ countries.
  • Tax penalty guarantee: ADP covers penalties from their tax filing errors. One of the strongest guarantees in the industry given ADP's scale.
  • Proactive compliance alerts (HR Pro): Legislative scanning with proactive alerts about new laws affecting your business, based on state and industry. Recommended actions included.
  • ACA compliance: Full ACA management — eligibility tracking, measurement periods, 1095-B/C generation and electronic filing, penalty risk assessment.
  • EEO reporting: EEO-1 report generation and diversity tracking across your workforce.
  • Wage and hour compliance: FLSA tracking, break/meal period compliance, overtime alerts.
  • I-9 and E-Verify: Electronic I-9 with E-Verify integration.
  • Workers' comp: Pay-as-you-go with real-time payroll data. Year-end audit preparation.
  • State tax registration: Available but may incur additional fees (unlike Gusto, which includes this free).

🏆 Verdict: Compliance

ADP wins for breadth — 140+ country coverage, comprehensive EEO reporting, and more sophisticated ACA management tools. For businesses in heavily regulated industries or with international operations, ADP's compliance infrastructure is deeper.

Gusto wins for value — automatic state tax registration at no extra charge is a genuine cost and time saver for growing businesses. When you hire your first employee in Texas or Florida, you don't need to research state requirements or pay $100+ for registration. Gusto handles it. For a standard U.S. small business, Gusto's compliance features cover everything you need.

🏆 Verdict: Integrations

That said, Gusto covers the integrations most small businesses actually need — QuickBooks, Xero, Slack, time tracking tools, and expense management. If your tech stack is QuickBooks + Slack + a time tracker, Gusto has you covered. The gap matters most for mid-market and enterprise businesses with specialized software needs.

Ease of Use & Onboarding

Gusto

  • Consumer-grade UX: Gusto's interface looks and feels like a modern consumer app. Clean design, intuitive navigation, minimal jargon. Business owners with zero HR experience can navigate it.
  • Self-service setup: Sign up online, add your business info, import employees, and run your first payroll — no sales call required. Typical setup takes 1-2 hours.
  • AutoPilot: Set and forget payroll for salaried employees. No other platform makes recurring payroll this effortless.
  • Mobile app: Well-designed for both admins and employees. Run payroll, approve PTO, view pay stubs — all from your phone.
  • Learning curve: Minimal. Most business owners are fully comfortable within a day. Gusto provides guided setup with inline help at every step.
  • Migration: Dedicated migration team helps import data from your previous provider (including ADP). Typically completes in 1-2 weeks.

ADP

  • Sales-driven onboarding: You must contact sales for a quote, negotiate pricing, and sign a contract before getting started. This process can take days or weeks.
  • ADP Run interface: Modernized and reasonably intuitive for basic payroll. Adequate for small businesses, though not as polished as Gusto.
  • Workforce Now interface: More complex — powerful but busier with more menus, options, and configuration. Requires training for admin users.
  • MyADP mobile app: Excellent for employee self-service (pay stubs, benefits, PTO). Admin mobile experience is adequate but not as clean.
  • Learning curve: ADP Run is moderate. Workforce Now has a steeper learning curve with 2-4 weeks to full proficiency. ADP Bridge offers training courses.
  • Implementation: ADP assigns an implementation team (for Workforce Now). Setup typically takes 2-6 weeks depending on complexity.

🏆 Verdict: Ease of Use

Gusto wins by a wide margin. From the moment you sign up (no sales call!) to the first payroll run (minutes, not days), Gusto is designed for business owners who aren't payroll professionals. AutoPilot payroll, inline guidance, and a consumer-grade interface make Gusto the easiest payroll platform available. ADP Run is adequate, but the sales-driven purchasing process and more traditional interface can't match Gusto's modern experience. If ease of use is your top priority, there's no contest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gusto or ADP better for small businesses?

For most small businesses with 1-100 employees, Gusto is the better choice. Transparent pricing ($49/month + $6/employee on Simple, $80/month + $12/employee on Plus), a modern interface, included benefits administration, and no hidden fees make Gusto purpose-built for SMBs. ADP is better for businesses expecting to scale past 100 employees, needing multi-country payroll, or requiring 500+ third-party integrations.

How much does Gusto cost compared to ADP in 2026?

Gusto publishes transparent pricing: Simple at $49/month + $6/employee, Plus at $80/month + $12/employee, Premium with custom pricing. ADP does not publish pricing — estimates suggest ADP Run Essential at $59-$79/month base + $4-$8/employee, but hidden fees (year-end processing, state registration, off-cycle runs) typically add 15-30% to the base cost. For a 20-employee business, Gusto Plus costs $3,840/year; ADP with comparable features costs approximately $5,320-$6,520/year.

Does Gusto include benefits administration?

Yes. Gusto includes benefits administration on all plans at no extra charge — health, dental, vision, life, disability insurance brokerage (licensed in all 50 states), 401(k) (via Guideline), HSA/FSA, commuter benefits, workers' comp, and COBRA. This is one of Gusto's biggest advantages over ADP, where benefits features often cost extra.

Can I switch from ADP to Gusto?

Yes. Gusto offers a dedicated migration team that imports your data from ADP, including employee records, tax information, and year-to-date payroll data. Best timing: beginning of a new year or quarter. Plan for 1-2 weeks of transition. Check your ADP contract for early termination fees — ADP contracts often auto-renew.

Does ADP have hidden fees that Gusto doesn't?

ADP is frequently criticized for undisclosed fees: year-end processing ($50-$200+), state tax registration, setup fees, off-cycle payroll charges, and annual 5-15% price increases at renewal. Gusto's pricing is all-inclusive: W-2/1099 filing, state registration, unlimited payroll runs, and year-end processing are included in the subscription.

Is ADP worth the extra cost over Gusto?

For specific use cases, yes: businesses with 100+ employees, international employees (140+ countries), advanced workforce analytics needs (DataCloud), or businesses requiring 500+ integrations. For a typical small business with 5-50 domestic employees, ADP's premium rarely provides proportional extra value.

Does Gusto handle multi-state payroll?

Yes. Gusto handles multi-state payroll across all 50 states and automatically registers your business for state tax withholding and unemployment insurance in new states — at no additional charge. ADP also handles multi-state payroll but often charges for new state registrations. Gusto does not support international payroll for employees (contractors in 80+ countries are supported).

What does Gusto do that ADP can't?

Gusto uniquely offers: transparent published pricing, unlimited payroll runs at no extra charge, free state tax registration, a dedicated contractor-only plan with automatic 1099 filing, Gusto Wallet (earned wage access + financial wellness), AutoPilot payroll for salaried employees, and built-in time tracking at no add-on cost. ADP charges separately for many of these capabilities.
