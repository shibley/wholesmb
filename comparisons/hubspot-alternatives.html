Why Look for HubSpot Alternatives?

HubSpot's free CRM is genuinely excellent — unlimited users, contact management for up to 1 million records, deal tracking, email logging, and live chat. For many small businesses, it's all they need.

The problem starts when you outgrow the free tier:

  • Pricing escalation — HubSpot Starter is $20/seat/month, but Professional jumps to $100/seat/month with a mandatory $1,500 onboarding fee. Enterprise is $150/seat/month with a $3,500 onboarding fee. A 15-person team on Professional costs $1,500/month + the initial onboarding fee — and that's before marketing contacts.
  • Marketing contact limits — HubSpot charges based on the number of marketing contacts in your database. Once you cross 2,000, costs climb quickly. At 50,000 contacts, you're looking at $2,300+/month for Marketing Hub Professional alone.
  • Feature gating — Critical features like A/B testing, custom reporting, calculated properties, and predictive lead scoring are locked behind Professional or Enterprise tiers. Many competitors include these at lower price points.
  • Annual contracts — HubSpot Professional and Enterprise require annual billing. You're locked in for 12 months with no monthly option.
  • Ecosystem lock-in — HubSpot works best when you use all of HubSpot. If you already have dedicated tools for marketing, support, or project management, paying for HubSpot's bundled approach means you're paying for overlap.

None of this makes HubSpot bad — it's still a top-tier platform. But if the pricing doesn't fit, or your needs are more specific than "everything in one place," there are CRMs that do certain things better for less money.

1. Zoho CRM — Best Overall HubSpot Alternative

If HubSpot is the CRM that tries to do everything, Zoho is the CRM that actually does everything — and charges less for it. Zoho CRM is the centerpiece of a massive ecosystem of 50+ business applications, from email and project management to accounting and HR. For small businesses looking for an all-in-one platform without HubSpot's pricing escalation, Zoho is the closest match.

What Makes Zoho CRM Stand Out

Zoho CRM's biggest advantage is value for money. The feature set at each price tier consistently undercuts HubSpot. Where HubSpot gates predictive lead scoring behind the $100/seat Professional tier, Zoho includes AI-powered lead scoring (via Zia AI) starting at Enterprise ($40/user/month). Where HubSpot charges extra for marketing contacts, Zoho's email marketing tools have no contact-based pricing layer.

The Zoho ecosystem is the real differentiator. With Zoho One ($35/employee/month), you get access to CRM, email marketing (Campaigns), help desk (Desk), project management (Projects), accounting (Books), HR (People), and 40+ more apps — all natively integrated. HubSpot charges separately for Marketing Hub, Service Hub, and Content Hub, each with their own tiered pricing.

Zoho's AI assistant Zia has also matured significantly. It predicts deal outcomes, suggests the best time to contact leads, detects anomalies in sales metrics, and can automate data enrichment. The new Zia Voice feature lets you query your CRM data with natural language, similar to HubSpot's Breeze AI.

Where Zoho falls short is UX polish. The interface is functional but not as clean or intuitive as HubSpot's. The sheer number of settings and customization options can feel overwhelming for non-technical users. And while Zoho's individual apps are good, they're not always as tightly integrated as HubSpot's native tools.

Pricing

  • Free: Up to 3 users — core CRM features, leads, contacts, deals, tasks
  • Standard: $14/user/month (annual) — workflows, cadences, custom reports, dashboards, sales forecasting
  • Professional: $23/user/month — CPQ, email intelligence, process automation, inventory management
  • Enterprise: $40/user/month — Zia AI, journey orchestration, territory management, custom functions, portals
  • Ultimate: $52/user/month — enhanced limits, consulting, data preparation, custom AI/ML models
  • Zoho One bundle: $35/employee/month — all 50+ Zoho apps

Zoho CRM vs HubSpot: Key Differences

  • Pricing: Zoho Enterprise ($40/user/mo) includes features HubSpot gates behind Professional ($100/seat/mo) and Enterprise ($150/seat/mo)
  • Marketing contacts: Zoho doesn't charge per marketing contact — HubSpot does, and costs escalate quickly
  • Ecosystem size: Zoho has 50+ native apps vs HubSpot's 6 hubs — Zoho covers more business functions
  • Ease of use: HubSpot wins here — cleaner UI, better onboarding, less overwhelming
  • Community & support: HubSpot Academy, community forums, and content library are industry-best. Zoho's support is adequate but less polished
  • Integrations: HubSpot's marketplace has 1,500+ integrations. Zoho Marketplace has 1,200+ but some categories are thinner

  • Free plan for up to 3 users

2. Pipedrive — Best HubSpot Alternative for Sales Teams

Pipedrive was built by salespeople who were tired of CRMs designed for managers. While HubSpot tries to be everything — marketing, sales, service, content — Pipedrive does one thing extraordinarily well: helping sales teams close deals. If your primary need is a sales CRM and HubSpot feels bloated, Pipedrive is your answer.

What Makes Pipedrive Stand Out

Pipedrive's visual sales pipeline is the best in the market. Deals are displayed as cards on a Kanban board that you can drag between stages. It sounds simple, but the execution is impeccable — every screen is designed around the question "what should I do next to close this deal?" HubSpot's pipeline view works similarly, but Pipedrive's feels more focused and less cluttered.

The activity-based selling methodology is built into the DNA. Instead of tracking vanity metrics, Pipedrive focuses on activities: calls made, emails sent, meetings scheduled. The system nudges you when deals go stale, suggests next steps, and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks. For sales reps, this is pure gold.

Pipedrive has also invested heavily in AI. The AI Sales Assistant analyzes your pipeline, spots at-risk deals, recommends actions, and even suggests optimal email send times. Smart Contact Data automatically enriches lead profiles from public sources. The AI CRM add-on ($8/user/month) adds email summarization, AI-generated follow-ups, and prospect research.

What Pipedrive doesn't have: native marketing automation, a help desk, or a content management system. It's a sales CRM, period. If you need email marketing, you'll need to integrate Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, or a similar tool. That's either a dealbreaker or a feature, depending on your philosophy.

Pricing

  • Essential: $14/user/month (annual) — visual pipeline, deal management, 3,000 open deals, custom fields, activity tracking
  • Advanced: $29/user/month — two-way email sync, group emailing, workflow automations (30 active), meeting scheduler
  • Professional: $49/user/month — AI Sales Assistant, contract & proposal management, revenue forecasting, custom dashboards
  • Power: $64/user/month — project management, phone support, 90 active automations, scalable permissions
  • Enterprise: $99/user/month — enhanced security, unlimited reports, unlimited open deals, dedicated account manager

Add-ons: LeadBooster ($32.50/mo), Web Visitors ($41/mo), Campaigns ($13.33/mo), Smart Docs ($32.50/mo), AI CRM ($8/user/mo).

Pipedrive vs HubSpot: Key Differences

  • Focus: Pipedrive is sales-only. HubSpot is an all-in-one platform. Choose based on what you actually need.
  • Pipeline UX: Pipedrive's pipeline management is more intuitive and sales-focused than HubSpot's
  • Pricing simplicity: Pipedrive charges per user, period. HubSpot layers user pricing + contact pricing + hub pricing
  • Marketing: HubSpot includes marketing natively. Pipedrive requires add-ons or integrations for email marketing
  • Free tier: HubSpot offers a generous free CRM. Pipedrive only offers a 14-day trial
  • Complexity: Pipedrive takes minutes to set up. HubSpot Professional can take days to configure properly

Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Best visual pipeline in any CRM
  • Extremely intuitive for sales teams
  • Activity-based selling methodology built-in
  • AI Sales Assistant that actually helps
  • Fast setup — minutes, not days
  • Transparent, predictable pricing

👎 Cons

  • No free plan (14-day trial only)
  • No native marketing automation — must integrate
  • No built-in help desk or customer support tools
  • Add-ons can increase cost significantly
  • Reporting less powerful than HubSpot or Salesforce

Best for: Sales-focused teams of 5-100 that want the cleanest pipeline management experience possible without paying for marketing and support tools they won't use.

3. Salesforce — Best HubSpot Alternative for Enterprise

Salesforce is the 800-pound gorilla of CRM. With over 150,000 customers and a 21% global market share, it's the platform that every other CRM is measured against. If HubSpot feels limiting because you've outgrown its customization capabilities or need deep enterprise features, Salesforce is the logical next step — though it comes with a significant jump in complexity and cost.

What Makes Salesforce Stand Out

Salesforce's core advantage is unlimited customization. Custom objects, custom fields, custom page layouts, complex automation with Flow Builder, Apex code for truly bespoke logic, and the AppExchange marketplace with 7,000+ integrations. If you can dream it, Salesforce can build it. HubSpot's customization options, while improving, still have ceilings that Salesforce doesn't.

The Einstein AI platform (now rebranded as Agentforce) is Salesforce's biggest bet. It goes beyond basic AI features — you can build autonomous AI agents that handle customer service inquiries, qualify leads, and even negotiate deals. Einstein Activity Capture automatically logs emails and meetings, Einstein Lead Scoring prioritizes your pipeline, and Einstein Copilot provides conversational AI across the platform.

Salesforce also launched a free tier in 2025 — the Free Suite supports up to 2 users with basic CRM, service case management, and simple email marketing. It's more limited than HubSpot's free CRM, but it's a sign that Salesforce is serious about competing at the SMB level.

The tradeoff is complexity. Salesforce requires more setup time, more training, and often a dedicated admin (or at least someone who's completed Salesforce Trailhead courses). Implementation can range from a few weeks for simple setups to months for enterprise rollouts. Most businesses under 20 employees will find Salesforce overkill.

Pricing

  • Free Suite: $0 — Up to 2 users, basic CRM, case management, simple email marketing
  • Starter Suite: $25/user/month (annual) — Full CRM suite, dynamic email marketing, built-in sales flows
  • Pro Suite: $100/user/month — Enhanced customization, automation, quoting, forecasting, AppExchange access
  • Enterprise: $165/user/month — Advanced pipeline management, conversation intelligence, deal insights
  • Unlimited: $330/user/month — Predictive AI, Einstein Copilot, Premier Success Plan included
  • Einstein 1 Sales: $500/user/month — Everything + Data Cloud, Revenue Intelligence, Slack integration

Note: Most add-on products (Marketing Cloud, Service Cloud, Commerce Cloud) are priced separately. Implementation consulting typically costs $5K-$50K+ depending on complexity.

Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Most customizable CRM platform on the market
  • 7,000+ AppExchange integrations
  • Agentforce AI platform for autonomous agents
  • Industry-specific solutions (healthcare, finance, manufacturing)
  • New free tier for up to 2 users
  • Largest CRM ecosystem and talent pool

👎 Cons

  • Steep learning curve — often needs a dedicated admin
  • Expensive: Pro Suite at $100/user is just the starting point
  • Hidden costs: implementation consulting, add-ons, training
  • UI is functional but not as clean as HubSpot or Pipedrive
  • Overkill for businesses under 20 employees
  • Annual contracts required for most plans

Best for: Mid-size to enterprise businesses that have outgrown HubSpot's customization limits, need complex sales workflows, or require industry-specific compliance features.

4. Freshsales (Freshworks) — Best Budget-Friendly HubSpot Alternative

Freshsales, part of the Freshworks ecosystem, is what you'd get if HubSpot and Pipedrive had a budget-conscious baby. It combines a clean, modern interface with built-in phone, email, and chat — all at prices that make HubSpot's paid tiers look expensive. For small businesses watching every dollar, Freshsales delivers a surprising amount of CRM for the money.

What Makes Freshsales Stand Out

Freshsales' biggest advantage is built-in communication channels. Phone, email, and live chat are native to the platform — no integrations, no add-ons, no extra cost. You can make calls, send emails, and chat with website visitors directly from deal and contact records. HubSpot offers similar capabilities, but some features (like calling minutes and live chat customization) require paid upgrades.

The Freddy AI assistant is available from the Pro tier ($39/user/month) and includes contact scoring, deal insights, next-best-action recommendations, and AI-powered email generation. For context, HubSpot's predictive lead scoring requires the Professional tier at $100/seat/month. Freshsales gives you comparable AI at 60% less.

Freshworks also offers Freshsales Suite — a combined CRM + marketing automation platform that's the closest direct competitor to HubSpot's bundled approach. It includes email marketing, landing pages, journey builder, and CRM in one product. Suite pricing starts at $11/user/month.

The free plan supports up to 3 users with basic contact management, built-in chat, email, and phone. It's not as generous as HubSpot's free CRM (which supports unlimited users), but for very small teams, it's a solid starting point.

Pricing

  • Free: Up to 3 users — contacts, accounts, deals, built-in chat, email, phone, mobile app
  • Growth: $9/user/month (annual) — visual pipeline, workflows, email templates, custom fields, Slack integration
  • Pro: $39/user/month — Freddy AI, multiple pipelines, sales sequences, territory management, custom reports
  • Enterprise: $59/user/month — custom modules, field-level permissions, audit logs, sandbox, AI forecasting

Freshsales Suite (CRM + Marketing): Free for 3 users, then $11/user/month (Growth), $47/user/month (Pro), $71/user/month (Enterprise).

Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Built-in phone, email, and chat — no add-ons needed
  • Freddy AI included at Pro tier (vs HubSpot at $100+/seat)
  • Clean, modern interface with fast onboarding
  • Freshsales Suite bundles CRM + marketing affordably
  • Free plan for up to 3 users
  • Part of larger Freshworks ecosystem (help desk, IT service, etc.)

👎 Cons

  • Smaller third-party integration ecosystem than HubSpot
  • Reporting is less advanced than HubSpot or Salesforce
  • Free plan limited to 3 users (HubSpot is unlimited)
  • Less well-known — smaller community and fewer tutorials
  • Some features require Freshsales Suite vs standalone Freshsales

Best for: Small businesses (3-50 employees) that want built-in communications, AI-powered deal insights, and a HubSpot-like experience at 50-70% lower cost.

5. ActiveCampaign — Best HubSpot Alternative for Marketing Automation

If you're leaving HubSpot primarily because of marketing automation limitations or pricing, ActiveCampaign should be at the top of your list. What started as an email marketing platform has evolved into a sophisticated CRM + marketing automation suite that outperforms HubSpot's automation capabilities at every price tier.

What Makes ActiveCampaign Stand Out

ActiveCampaign's automation builder is its crown jewel. The visual workflow editor lets you create complex, branching automation sequences with conditions, wait steps, goals, split actions, and webhooks. You can trigger automations based on virtually any event — email opens, page visits, form submissions, deal stage changes, custom field updates, or even date-based triggers. HubSpot offers similar workflow capabilities, but only at Professional ($800/month for 2,000 contacts) and above.

With ActiveCampaign, you get access to automations from the Starter tier at $15/month. The gap between what HubSpot charges for automation and what ActiveCampaign charges is enormous — easily the biggest pricing differential on this list.

Machine learning is baked into the platform. Predictive sending optimizes email delivery times per recipient. Win probability scores deals based on historical patterns. Sentiment analysis gauges contact engagement. And the AI-powered content generator helps craft emails and subject lines.

The CRM component (available on Plus plan and above) is decent but not best-in-class. It handles deal tracking, pipeline management, and sales automation, but it's not as polished as Pipedrive's sales-focused interface. Think of ActiveCampaign as marketing-first, CRM-second — the inverse of most platforms on this list.

Pricing

  • Starter: $15/month for 1,000 contacts — email marketing, automation, 900+ templates, inline forms, site tracking
  • Plus: $49/month for 1,000 contacts — CRM, landing pages, lead scoring, conditional content, SMS marketing
  • Pro: $79/month for 1,000 contacts — predictive sending, attribution, split automations, site messages
  • Enterprise: $145/month for 1,000 contacts — custom objects, custom reporting, unlimited emails, HIPAA compliance

Pricing scales with contacts. 10,000 contacts: Starter $79/mo, Plus $149/mo. 50,000 contacts: Starter $239/mo, Plus $379/mo. Significantly cheaper than HubSpot at every tier.

Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Most powerful automation builder for the price
  • Automation available from $15/month (HubSpot: $800+/month)
  • Predictive sending and machine learning built-in
  • 900+ pre-built automation templates
  • Excellent email deliverability rates
  • 950+ integrations including Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress

👎 Cons

  • CRM is functional but not best-in-class for pure sales teams
  • No free plan — only 14-day trial
  • Interface can feel complex for beginners
  • Reporting not as polished as HubSpot
  • No native help desk or customer service tools

Best for: Marketing-driven businesses that rely heavily on email automation, lead nurturing, and multi-step campaigns. If HubSpot's automation pricing shocked you, ActiveCampaign delivers more for dramatically less.

6. Monday CRM — Best HubSpot Alternative for Project-Heavy Teams

Monday.com started as a work management platform and expanded into CRM territory. The result is a uniquely flexible system that blends project management, sales tracking, and workflow automation into a single interface. For teams where deal management and project delivery are tightly linked — like agencies, consulting firms, and professional services — Monday CRM bridges a gap that HubSpot doesn't naturally fill.

What Makes Monday CRM Stand Out

Monday CRM's superpower is flexibility. The platform is built on a board-and-column system that you can configure for virtually anything: sales pipelines, project timelines, content calendars, client onboarding workflows, or inventory tracking. Where HubSpot has a fixed CRM structure (contacts → companies → deals), Monday lets you build whatever data model fits your business.

The handoff from sales to delivery is seamless. When a deal closes, you can automatically create a project board, assign tasks to delivery team members, and kick off an onboarding workflow — all within the same platform. In HubSpot, this requires integrating a separate project management tool (Asana, Monday, ClickUp) and building automation bridges.

Monday's AI Sidekick helps generate email drafts, summarize activities, build formulas, and analyze data. Automations are visual and easy to build, though the automation cap on Standard (250/month) can be limiting for heavy users — HubSpot's Starter has a similar cap at 10 automation actions/contact/enrollment.

Pricing

  • Standard: $17/seat/month — email integration, AI features, activity management, quotes, 250 automations/month
  • Pro: $28/seat/month — sales forecasting, mass emails, custom dashboards, 25,000 automations/month
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing — lead scoring, advanced permissions, HIPAA compliance, unlimited automations

Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Best CRM for teams that also need project management
  • Extremely flexible board/column data model
  • Seamless sales-to-delivery handoff
  • Beautiful, colorful interface that teams enjoy using
  • Microsoft Copilot integration for AI capabilities
  • No contact-based pricing layer

👎 Cons

  • Minimum 3 seats ($36/month minimum)
  • No free CRM plan (only 14-day trial)
  • CRM features less mature than HubSpot or Salesforce
  • Automation cap on Standard tier (250/month)
  • No native marketing automation or email marketing
  • Per-seat pricing adds up for larger teams

Best for: Agencies, consulting firms, and service businesses that need CRM + project management in one tool. If your team manages both selling and delivering work, Monday CRM eliminates the gap between the two.

7. Copper — Best HubSpot Alternative for Google Workspace Users

If your business lives inside Google Workspace — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets — Copper is the CRM that meets you where you already work. Rather than pulling you into a separate application, Copper embeds itself directly into Gmail as a sidebar, letting you manage contacts, track deals, and log activities without ever leaving your inbox.

What Makes Copper Stand Out

Copper's Google Workspace integration is the deepest of any CRM on the market. It's an official Google-recommended CRM, and the integration shows. Emails are automatically logged, calendar events are synced, files from Google Drive are attached to records, and you can create and update CRM records directly from Gmail. The experience feels less like using a CRM and more like Gmail gained superpowers.

Automatic data entry is another major advantage. Copper captures contact information from emails, enriches profiles from public data, and suggests new contacts to add based on your communication history. For teams that hate manual data entry (which is most teams), this is transformative. HubSpot offers email logging and some automation, but Copper's zero-input approach is more aggressive.

The pipeline management is clean and visual, with customizable stages and simple drag-and-drop deal tracking. Reporting is adequate for small teams, with pre-built dashboards for pipeline, revenue, and activity metrics. The new AI features help with email generation and deal insights.

Pricing

  • Starter: $9/user/month (annual) — up to 1,000 contacts, Google Workspace integration, pipelines, basic reporting
  • Basic: $23/user/month — 15,000 contacts, task automation, pipeline automations, activity feed
  • Professional: $59/user/month — 50,000 contacts, workflow automation, bulk email, custom reporting
  • Business: $99/user/month — unlimited contacts, advanced reporting, goal tracking, email sequences

Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Deepest Google Workspace integration of any CRM
  • Automatic data capture from emails — minimal manual entry
  • Official Google-recommended CRM
  • Beautiful, modern interface that feels like a Google product
  • Fast setup — works inside Gmail immediately

👎 Cons

  • Only works with Google Workspace — no Microsoft 365 support
  • Contact limits on lower tiers (1,000 on Starter)
  • Limited marketing automation capabilities
  • Reporting less sophisticated than HubSpot
  • No free plan (14-day trial only)

Best for: Google Workspace-native businesses (agencies, consultancies, startups) that want a CRM that doesn't feel like a separate tool. If your team lives in Gmail, Copper is the most natural HubSpot alternative.

8. Insightly — Best HubSpot Alternative for Project Delivery

While Monday CRM approaches this from the project management side, Insightly comes at it from the CRM side — with deal tracking as the foundation and project delivery as a natural extension.

What Makes Insightly Stand Out

Insightly's linked relationships model is its standout feature. Every contact, organization, project, and opportunity can be connected to every other record through relationship links. This creates a rich web of connections that helps you understand how your contacts, companies, and deals relate to each other. For businesses with complex relationship networks (real estate, B2B services, consulting), this is more powerful than HubSpot's standard contact → company → deal hierarchy.

The project management module lets you convert won opportunities directly into projects with milestones, tasks, pipelines, and activity tracking. Project records maintain all the relationship links from the original deal, so your delivery team has full context. No separate tool needed, no data re-entry.

Insightly also offers Insightly Marketing and Insightly Service as add-on modules, creating a more complete platform. AppConnect, the built-in integration builder, lets you create no-code integrations with 500+ apps.

Pros & Cons

9. Folk CRM — Best HubSpot Alternative for Relationship-First Teams

Folk is the modern, minimalist CRM for teams that think about customer relationships first and sales processes second. It's popular with VCs, recruiting firms, startups, and agencies — teams that manage networks of people rather than strict sales funnels. If HubSpot feels like bringing a battleship to a canoe race, Folk is the canoe: light, fast, and surprisingly capable.

What Makes Folk Stand Out

Folk's contact-centric design rethinks CRM from the ground up. Instead of forcing contacts into a rigid deal pipeline, Folk lets you organize people into flexible groups, tag them freely, and manage relationships across multiple contexts — sales, partnerships, investors, hiring, personal network. One person can exist in multiple groups with different contexts, which mirrors how relationships actually work.

The Chrome extension and browser enrichment are exceptionally good. You can add contacts from LinkedIn, Twitter, Gmail, or any website with one click. Folk automatically enriches profiles with company data, social links, and contact details. The magic is in the speed — building a CRM of 500 contacts from LinkedIn takes minutes, not hours.

Folk also includes email sequences for outreach and follow-ups, a clean pipeline view for deals, and integrations with common tools. The interface is beautiful — minimal, fast, and a joy to use. Think Notion meets CRM.

Pricing

  • Standard: $20/member/month (annual) — 5,000 contacts, email sequences, AI enrichment, integrations
  • Premium: $40/member/month — 50,000 contacts, advanced analytics, priority support, API access
  • Custom: Enterprise pricing — unlimited contacts, dedicated support, custom integrations

Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Beautiful, modern interface that's a joy to use
  • Flexible contact organization — not locked into rigid pipelines
  • Excellent Chrome extension for one-click contact capture
  • AI-powered enrichment for fast data building
  • Free plan available (100 contacts)
  • Great for relationship-heavy roles (VCs, recruiters, agencies)

👎 Cons

  • Limited advanced CRM features (no custom objects, limited reporting)
  • Not suited for complex enterprise sales processes

  • Free plan limited to 100 contacts

Best for: VCs, recruiting firms, startups, and agencies that manage relationship networks rather than traditional sales funnels. If your CRM is more Rolodex than revenue machine, Folk is refreshingly simple.

10. Capsule CRM — Best Simple, No-Fuss HubSpot Alternative

Not every business needs a feature-packed CRM platform. Some need a clean, straightforward tool to track contacts, manage a sales pipeline, and log activities — without drowning in features they'll never use. Capsule CRM is that tool. It's been quietly serving small businesses since 2009, and its philosophy is simple: do the basics exceptionally well.

What Makes Capsule Stand Out

Capsule's appeal is simplicity without sacrifice. The interface is clean and uncluttered. Contact management is intuitive. The sales pipeline is visual and easy to manage. Tasks and activities are tracked without complexity. There are no 47 sub-menus or 200 settings pages to configure. You sign up, import contacts, and start working.

Despite the simplicity, Capsule includes features that matter: workflow automation (Tracks) that guides your team through repeatable processes, custom fields and tags for organizing contacts your way, AI content assistant for email drafting, and solid integrations with tools like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Mailchimp, Xero, QuickBooks, and Zapier.

Capsule's free tier supports 2 users and 250 contacts — modest, but enough for a solopreneur or two-person team to test the waters. Paid plans scale from $18 to $72/user/month, which is competitive for what you get.

Pricing

  • Free: 2 users, 250 contacts, 1 sales pipeline, 1 project board, AI content assistant
  • Starter: $18/user/month — 30,000 contacts, 1 pipeline, 1 project board, premium integrations
  • Growth: $36/user/month — 60,000 contacts, 5 pipelines, 5 project boards, workflow automation, advanced reporting
  • Advanced: $54/user/month — 120,000 contacts, 50 pipelines, 50 project boards, custom fields on activities

The Bottom Line: Which HubSpot Alternative Should You Choose?

The "best" HubSpot alternative depends entirely on why you're looking for one:

  • Want HubSpot's features at lower cost? → Zoho CRM. The closest all-in-one platform at 40-60% less. The Zoho One bundle ($35/employee/month) is the best value in CRM.
  • Need a pure sales CRM? → Pipedrive. Best visual pipeline, fastest setup, most intuitive for salespeople. Stop paying for marketing tools you don't use.
  • Outgrown HubSpot's customization? → Salesforce. Unlimited customization, 7,000+ integrations, industry-specific solutions. Be prepared for higher complexity and cost.
  • On a tight budget? → Freshsales. Built-in phone, email, and chat from $9/user/month. Freddy AI at $39 beats HubSpot's AI at $100.
  • Marketing automation is the priority? → ActiveCampaign. More powerful automations than HubSpot at every tier, starting at $15/month.
  • Manage projects and deals together? → Monday CRM. Best for agencies and service businesses that need sales + project management in one tool.
  • All-in on Google Workspace? → Copper. Lives inside Gmail. The most native Google CRM experience available.
  • Just want something simple? → Capsule CRM. Does the basics well without drowning you in features. Sign up, import contacts, start working.

One final thought: HubSpot's free CRM is still excellent. If you're not hitting its limitations, there's no rush to switch. But if you're staring at a $1,500/month bill for Professional features that Zoho or Freshsales offer at $500/month, the math speaks for itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best alternative to HubSpot for small businesses?

For most small businesses, Zoho CRM is the best overall HubSpot alternative. It offers a comparable all-in-one platform (CRM, marketing, support, analytics) at roughly 40-60% less than HubSpot's paid plans. The free tier supports 3 users, Standard starts at $14/user/month, and the entire Zoho ecosystem (50+ apps) integrates natively. If you need pure sales-focused CRM without marketing bloat, Pipedrive is excellent at $14/user/month with a best-in-class visual pipeline.

Is HubSpot worth the price?

HubSpot's free CRM is genuinely excellent and worth using. The paid plans are where it gets complicated. Starter ($20/seat/month) is reasonable for growing businesses. But Professional ($100/seat/month with a $1,500 onboarding fee) and Enterprise ($150/seat/month with a $3,500 onboarding fee) can get expensive fast, especially when you add marketing contacts. For teams under 20 people, alternatives like Zoho, Pipedrive, or Freshsales offer 80% of HubSpot's functionality at 40-70% lower cost.

Can I switch from HubSpot to another CRM easily?

Yes, most modern CRMs offer HubSpot migration tools or import wizards. You can export your HubSpot contacts, deals, companies, and notes as CSV files and import them into any alternative. Salesforce, Zoho, and Pipedrive all have dedicated HubSpot migration guides. The trickiest parts are recreating complex workflows/automations and moving email templates. Plan for 1-2 weeks of setup and testing for a team of 10-20 users.

What is the cheapest HubSpot alternative with similar features?

Zoho CRM offers the most HubSpot-like feature set at the lowest price. The free plan covers 3 users with core CRM features. Standard is $14/user/month (annual), and Professional at $23/user/month includes CPQ, process automation, and inventory management. The Zoho One bundle ($35/employee/month) gives you access to 50+ apps — essentially everything HubSpot offers and more, at a fraction of the cost.

Does HubSpot have a free plan?

Yes, HubSpot's free CRM is one of the best free CRM tools available. It includes unlimited users, contact management (up to 1,000,000 contacts), deal tracking, email tracking (200 notifications/month), meeting scheduling, live chat, basic reporting, and integrations. The limitations are primarily around automation (no workflows), advanced reporting, and marketing features. Many small businesses use HubSpot's free CRM for years before needing to upgrade.

Which HubSpot alternative is best for sales teams?

Pipedrive is the best HubSpot alternative for pure sales teams. It was built by salespeople, and it shows — the visual pipeline interface is the most intuitive in the market, deal management is streamlined, and activity-based selling methodology keeps reps focused. At $14-$64/user/month, it's significantly cheaper than HubSpot's Sales Hub Professional.

Which HubSpot alternative has the best marketing automation?

ActiveCampaign is the strongest HubSpot alternative for marketing automation specifically. Its visual automation builder is more powerful than HubSpot's at every price tier, with conditional logic, split testing, goals, and multi-channel sequences built in from the start. Pricing starts at $15/month for 1,000 contacts — a fraction of HubSpot's Marketing Hub Professional at $800/month.

Is Salesforce better than HubSpot?

Salesforce is more powerful and customizable than HubSpot, but also more complex and expensive. For small businesses under 50 employees with straightforward sales processes, HubSpot is typically the better choice. For larger businesses with complex sales processes, multiple product lines, or specific compliance requirements, Salesforce's depth of customization makes it worth the investment.
