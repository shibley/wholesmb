Gusto vs BambooHR (2026): Payroll-First vs HR-First — Which Platform Does Your Business Actually Need?

Gusto and BambooHR are built around fundamentally different philosophies. Gusto is a payroll-first platform that added HR features — transparent pricing, everything included, designed to make paying people dead simple. BambooHR is an HR-first platform that added payroll — a comprehensive HRIS built for managing the full employee lifecycle, with payroll available as an add-on. We've compared both across pricing, payroll, HR capabilities, performance management, benefits, and more with current 2026 data.

⚡ Quick Verdict

🟢 Choose Gusto If…

Payroll is your primary need and you want everything included at one transparent price. Gusto starts at $49/month + $6/employee with full-service payroll, automatic tax filing, benefits brokerage, and unlimited payroll runs — all built in, no add-ons required. You can sign up and run your first payroll in 1-2 days without a sales call. Best for US-based businesses with 1-100 employees that want payroll to just work without thinking about it.

🔵 Choose BambooHR If…

You need a comprehensive HRIS that goes beyond payroll — performance management, employee experience, advanced reporting, and recruiting. BambooHR is built around the employee lifecycle: hiring, onboarding, managing, developing, and offboarding. Payroll is available as an add-on but isn't the core product. Best for businesses with 25-350 employees that need to professionalize their HR operations and care as much about people management as they do about paying people.

💡 The honest truth: This isn't really an apples-to-apples comparison. Gusto is primarily a payroll platform that added HR. BambooHR is primarily an HRIS that added payroll. If your main problem is "I need to pay my employees correctly and on time," Gusto solves it more directly and more affordably. If your main problem is "I need a system to manage employee records, performance reviews, and the full hire-to-retire lifecycle," BambooHR goes deeper. Some businesses even use both — BambooHR as the HRIS, Gusto as the payroll engine — because the two integrate with each other.

Overview: Two Different Starting Points

The Gusto vs BambooHR comparison reveals what happens when two platforms solve the same broad problem — managing your workforce — but start from completely different places.

Gusto was born as a payroll company. Founded in 2011 as ZenPayroll, the platform was built around a single premise: small businesses shouldn't need an accountant or payroll specialist to pay their people. Gusto made payroll simple, transparent, and automatic — then gradually expanded into benefits, HR tools, hiring, time tracking, and compliance. But payroll remains the beating heart of the product. As of 2026, Gusto serves over 300,000 businesses and processes payroll for more than 6 million employees across all 50 US states. The company has raised over $746 million in funding.

BambooHR was born as an HR information system. Founded in 2008 in Lindon, Utah by Ben Peterson and Ryan Sanders, BambooHR was built to replace the spreadsheets, filing cabinets, and scattered records that small businesses used to manage their people. The platform started with employee records, PTO tracking, and reporting — the unglamorous but essential infrastructure of HR. Payroll came later, through the acquisition of Trax Payroll, and was integrated as an add-on module rather than a core feature. As of 2026, BambooHR serves more than 33,000 companies with employees in over 190 countries and 50 industries. The company generated approximately $274 million in revenue and has grown primarily through bootstrapped profitability.

These different origins create meaningfully different products in 2026:

  • Gusto excels at payroll processing, tax compliance, benefits brokerage, and financial tools for employees (Gusto Wallet, Gusto Cashout). Its HR features are solid but secondary.
  • BambooHR excels at employee data management, performance reviews, engagement surveys, applicant tracking, and the overall employee experience. Its payroll is functional but secondary.

The competitive landscape has also diverged. Gusto competes primarily against payroll platforms — ADP, Paychex, and OnPay — while positioning itself as simpler and more transparent. BambooHR competes primarily against HRIS platforms — Namely, Bob (HiBob), Paylocity, and Zenefits — while positioning itself as the most intuitive and best-supported option for growing businesses.

The question isn't which is "better" — it's which problem are you solving first? If the answer is "payroll," start with Gusto. If the answer is "HR," start with BambooHR.

What Is Gusto?

Gusto is a cloud-based payroll, benefits, and HR platform headquartered in San Francisco and Denver. Founded in 2011 by Josh Reeves, Edward Kim, and Tomer London, Gusto has raised over $746 million in venture funding with an approximate valuation of $9.5 billion. The platform serves over 300,000 businesses with a sweet spot of 1-200 employees.

    Full-service payroll, automatic tax filing in all 50 states, employee self-service, basic hiring and onboarding, health insurance administration, workers' compensation, and integrations. Best for single-state businesses with straightforward payroll.
  • Plus — $80/month + $12/employee/month
    Everything in Simple plus: multi-state payroll, full HR suite (employee directory, org chart, custom permissions), built-in time tracking and PTO management, project tracking, workforce costing, applicant tracking, performance reviews, employee surveys, and advanced onboarding checklists.
  • Premium — Custom pricing (25+ employees)
    Everything in Plus, plus: dedicated customer success manager, HR resource center with compliance alerts, certified HR experts, custom admin permissions, priority support, and migration assistance.
  • Contractor-only — $35/month + $6/contractor/month
    Pay contractors via direct deposit, automatic 1099 filing, contractor self-service portal, international contractor payments in 80+ countries.

Critical detail: Every Gusto plan includes unlimited payroll runs (including off-cycle, bonus, and correction runs), automatic federal/state/local tax filing, W-2 and 1099 preparation, direct deposit, and benefits brokerage — at no extra charge. No setup fees. No implementation fees. No year-end surprise bills.

Gusto Core Capabilities

  • Full-service payroll: Automated payroll with next-day or 2-day direct deposit. AutoPilot mode runs payroll automatically for salaried employees. Handles hourly, salaried, tipped, and commission workers. Multiple pay schedules. Unlimited runs.
  • Tax filing: Calculates, files, and remits all federal, state, and local payroll taxes automatically. Quarterly (941), annual (940, W-2, W-3, 1099). Free state tax registration in new states. Tax penalty protection included.
  • Benefits brokerage: Licensed insurance broker in all 50 states. Health, dental, vision, life, and disability from multiple carriers. 401(k) through Guideline. HSAs, FSAs, commuter benefits, 529 college savings, and pay-as-you-go workers' comp.

  • Hiring & onboarding: Job posting, offer letters, digital document signing, I-9 and W-4 collection, E-Verify, custom onboarding checklists.
  • Time tracking (Plus/Premium): Built-in time tracking with GPS, mobile clock-in, PTO management, project tracking, overtime calculations — flows directly to payroll.
  • HR tools (Plus/Premium): Employee directory, org chart, custom reporting, performance reviews, employee surveys, document storage, compliance alerts.
  • International contractors: Pay contractors in 80+ countries. Does not support international full-time employees or EOR services.
  • Gusto Embedded: Payroll API for other platforms to embed Gusto's payroll engine into their own products — used by accounting firms and vertical SaaS companies.

What Is BambooHR?

BambooHR is a cloud-based human resources information system (HRIS) headquartered in Lindon, Utah. Founded in 2008 by Ben Peterson and Ryan Sanders, BambooHR has grown to serve over 33,000 companies with employees in more than 190 countries. Notably, BambooHR has been primarily bootstrapped — generating approximately $274 million in annual revenue without the massive venture capital funding that characterizes many HR tech companies. This bootstrapped discipline shows in the product: BambooHR focuses on doing HR well rather than trying to be everything to everyone.

BambooHR Plans (2026 Pricing)

BambooHR does not publish pricing on its website — you must contact sales for a quote. Based on industry data and published estimates:

  • Core (formerly Essentials) — ~$5-$7/employee/month
    HR data and reporting, hiring and onboarding, time-off tracking, benefits tracking, employee self-service, employee directory, and standard integrations. Flat monthly rate for companies under 25 employees; per-employee pricing above 25. Best for smaller teams that need an organized employee database.
  • Pro (formerly Advantage) — ~$8-$11/employee/month
    Everything in Core plus: performance management (goal tracking, reviews, 360° feedback), employee experience and engagement surveys (eNPS), employee community features, and advanced customization. Best for growing companies that need to manage employee development and culture.

Add-on modules (additional cost):

  • Payroll — $150/month for up to 20 employees, ~$7.25/employee after
    Full-service payroll with direct deposit, flexible pay schedules, federal and state tax filing, and a specialized payroll support team. Built on acquired Trax Payroll technology.
  • Benefits Administration — additional cost (quote required)
    Self-guided benefits configuration, open enrollment management, life event handling, carrier connections, and reporting.
  • Time Tracking — additional cost (quote required)
    Mobile and desktop time clock, project tracking, billable hours, employee timesheets, automated approval workflows, and automatic overtime calculations.

Volume discounts apply as your employee count grows. Companies under 25 employees get a flat monthly rate. BambooHR also offers a discount when you bundle payroll and benefits with either the Core or Pro plan. Implementation fees typically run 5-15% of annual software fees.

BambooHR Core Capabilities

  • Employee records & database: Centralized employee profiles with personal information, employment history, compensation data, documents, and custom fields. This is BambooHR's foundation — it's one of the best employee databases in the SMB market.
  • Applicant tracking system (ATS): Full recruiting pipeline — job postings, candidate management, application tracking, interview scheduling, offer letters, and automatic conversion from candidate to employee upon hire.
  • Onboarding & offboarding: Pre-boarding task lists, electronic document signing, new hire packets, IT provisioning checklists, and structured offboarding with exit interviews and equipment return tracking.
  • Performance management (Pro): Goal setting and tracking, scheduled and ad-hoc performance reviews, 360° feedback, manager and peer assessments, and performance improvement plans. One of BambooHR's strongest differentiators.
  • Employee experience (Pro): Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS) surveys, employee well-being assessments, anonymous feedback channels, and engagement trend reporting. Helps HR leaders measure and improve company culture.
  • Time-off management: PTO accrual tracking, leave request and approval workflows, leave balance visibility, holiday calendars, and customizable PTO policies. Included in all plans.
  • Payroll (add-on): Full-service payroll processing with direct deposit, multiple pay schedules, automatic federal and state tax filing, and year-end tax document preparation (W-2s, 1099s). Integrates with BambooHR's time tracking for automatic hours import.
  • Benefits tracking & administration: Core plans include basic benefits tracking (enrollment records, plan details). The benefits administration add-on enables open enrollment, life event changes, carrier connections, and benefits reporting.
  • Reporting & analytics: Pre-built reports for headcount, turnover, time-to-hire, compensation analysis, and EEO compliance. Custom report builder for ad-hoc analysis. Workforce planning data visualizations.
  • Employee self-service: Employees can view their profiles, update personal information, request time off, access pay stubs, view the company directory, and complete assigned tasks — all via web or mobile app.
  • Training tracking: Track employee certifications, compliance courses, training completion dates, and renewal deadlines. Useful for regulated industries and compliance-heavy businesses.

Pricing Compared: All-Inclusive vs Modular Add-Ons

Pricing is where the "payroll-first vs HR-first" philosophy becomes most visible — and most frustrating to compare.

Gusto: Everything Included, Price on the Label

Gusto's pricing is designed for people who hate surprises. Every price is published. Every plan includes payroll, tax filing, benefits brokerage, and direct deposit. There are no add-on modules, no implementation fees, and no year-end processing charges.

For a business with 25 employees:

  • Simple plan: $49 + (25 × $6) = $199/month — includes full payroll, tax filing, benefits
  • Plus plan: $80 + (25 × $12) = $380/month — adds HR suite, time tracking, performance reviews

No hidden costs. Want to run an extra bonus payroll? Included. File taxes in a new state? Included. Administer health insurance? Included. This is Gusto's competitive advantage: predictability.

BambooHR: Choose What You Need, Pay for Each Piece

BambooHR's pricing is modular — you pick a base plan (Core or Pro) and add modules based on your needs. This can be cost-effective if you only need HR tools without payroll, but it gets complex when you start adding everything.

For a business with 25 employees (estimated based on industry data):

  • Core plan only: ~25 × $6 = ~$150/month — HR records, hiring, onboarding, time-off tracking
  • Pro plan only: ~25 × $10 = ~$250/month — adds performance management, engagement surveys
  • Pro + Payroll: ~$250 + $150 + (5 × $7.25) = ~$436/month
  • Pro + Payroll + Benefits + Time Tracking: estimated $500-$700/month

Plus implementation fees of approximately 5-15% of annual costs — so for a $6,000/year software spend, expect $300-$900 in one-time setup costs.

The Pricing Truth Table

The fair comparison depends on what you're buying:

  • Just payroll + benefits: Gusto Simple ($199/mo) vs BambooHR Core + Payroll + Benefits (~$350-$400/mo). Gusto wins by ~50%.
  • Full HR + payroll + benefits + time tracking: Gusto Plus ($380/mo) vs BambooHR Pro + all add-ons (~$500-$700/mo). Gusto wins by 30-45%.
  • Just HRIS (no payroll): Gusto doesn't offer this. BambooHR Core ($150/mo) or Pro ($250/mo). BambooHR wins by default.
  • HR depth (performance, engagement, surveys): BambooHR Pro is significantly deeper than Gusto Plus in these areas. BambooHR wins on features.

Bottom line: If you need payroll + basic HR, Gusto is almost always cheaper. If you need deep HRIS capabilities and can live with payroll as an add-on (or use a separate payroll provider), BambooHR offers more HR depth for the dollar.

Payroll Processing & Tax Filing

This is where Gusto's "payroll-first" DNA becomes most apparent.

Gusto Payroll

Payroll is what Gusto was built for, and it shows. Key advantages:

  • Unlimited payroll runs on every plan — including off-cycle, bonus, correction, and commission runs. No extra charges, ever.
  • AutoPilot mode: For salaried employees with no variable pay, Gusto can run payroll completely automatically. Set it and forget it.
  • Next-day or 2-day direct deposit — no waiting a week for paychecks to clear.
  • Automatic tax filing: Federal, state, and local taxes calculated, filed, and remitted on your behalf. Quarterly returns, W-2s, W-3s, 1099s — all handled. Tax penalty protection if Gusto makes an error.
  • Free state registration: When you hire in a new state, Gusto handles the tax registration at no additional cost.
  • Contractor payments: Pay domestic and international contractors (80+ countries) with automatic 1099 filing.
  • Pay-as-you-go workers' comp: Integrated with payroll so premiums adjust automatically based on actual payroll data — no large upfront deposits.

Gusto's payroll experience is designed for non-accountants. The interface walks you through each step, flags anomalies (unusual hours, missing information), and provides a clear summary before you approve. Most users spend less than 10 minutes per payroll run.

BambooHR Payroll

BambooHR's payroll is functional and improving, but it's an add-on — not the core product:

  • Full-service payroll with direct deposit and multiple pay schedules.
  • Federal and state tax filing — automatic calculation, filing, and remittance.
  • Year-end tax documents: W-2 preparation and distribution.
  • Dedicated payroll support team separate from general BambooHR support.
  • Integration with BambooHR time tracking: Hours flow directly from the time tracking add-on into payroll calculations.

BambooHR's payroll was built on the Trax Payroll acquisition and has been gradually improved. It handles the basics well — standard payroll runs, tax filing, direct deposit — but lacks some of Gusto's refinements. There's no AutoPilot mode, no pay-as-you-go workers' comp integration, no contractor payment capabilities, no early wage access, and the payroll interface isn't as polished or intuitive as Gusto's.

Payroll Verdict

Gusto wins decisively on payroll. It's the better payroll product by a meaningful margin — faster to set up, easier to use, more features included, and cheaper when you factor in BambooHR's add-on pricing. If payroll is your primary need, this section alone should steer your decision. BambooHR's payroll is adequate for businesses that choose BambooHR for its HR capabilities and want payroll under the same roof, but it shouldn't be the reason you choose BambooHR.

HR & Employee Records

This is where BambooHR's "HR-first" DNA takes over.

BambooHR HR

BambooHR was built as an employee database first — and it remains one of the best in the SMB market:

  • Comprehensive employee profiles: Personal info, employment history, compensation data, documents, training records, performance reviews, benefits enrollment, and custom fields — all in one record.
  • Org chart: Automatic visual organization chart that updates as you add employees and adjust reporting structures.
  • Employee directory: Searchable company directory with photos, departments, locations, and contact information. Employees can find each other without asking HR.
  • Custom access levels: Three tiers (employee, manager, custom) with granular permissions. Control exactly who sees what — critical for growing organizations with sensitive data.
  • Document management: Store employment agreements, signed policies, certifications, performance reviews, and any other documents attached to employee records. E-signature capability for new documents.
  • Training tracking: Monitor certifications, compliance courses, training completions, and renewal deadlines. Essential for regulated industries (healthcare, finance, construction).
  • Employee community (Pro): Company announcements, celebrations (birthdays, work anniversaries), and team communication features.

Gusto HR

Gusto's HR tools are solid but clearly secondary to payroll:

  • Employee profiles: Basic employee records with personal info, employment data, pay history, and tax documents. Less customizable than BambooHR.
  • Employee directory (Plus/Premium): Searchable directory with employee information and org chart.
  • Document storage: Store and manage employee documents with e-signature capabilities.
  • Custom permissions (Plus/Premium): Role-based access control, though less granular than BambooHR's three-tier system.
  • Compliance alerts (Premium): Notifications about regulatory changes, required postings, and compliance deadlines.
  • HR resource center (Premium): Access to compliance guides, HR templates, and certified HR professionals for advice.

HR Verdict

BambooHR wins on HR depth. BambooHR's employee database is more comprehensive, more customizable, and better suited for organizations that need to manage complex employee data across departments, locations, and access levels. Gusto's HR features are sufficient for small businesses that primarily need payroll but want basic employee management, but they don't match BambooHR's purpose-built HRIS capabilities. If you need to track certifications, manage training compliance, or maintain detailed employment histories, BambooHR is the stronger choice.

Performance Management

This is one of the biggest differentiators between the two platforms — and where BambooHR's HR-first approach pays off most clearly.

BambooHR Performance Management (Pro Plan)

BambooHR offers a dedicated performance management suite that's among the best in the SMB HRIS market:

  • Goal setting and tracking: Employees and managers can set, track, and update goals collaboratively. Goals can be aligned to team or company objectives for visibility across the organization.
  • Performance reviews: Flexible review cycles — annual, semi-annual, quarterly, or ad-hoc. Customizable review templates with self-assessments, manager evaluations, and peer feedback.
  • 360° feedback: Collect feedback from managers, peers, direct reports, and cross-functional collaborators for a complete performance picture.
  • Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS): Regular pulse surveys that measure employee satisfaction and engagement over time. Track trends and identify issues before they become problems.
  • Employee well-being assessments: Surveys designed to gauge employee sentiment, work-life balance, and overall satisfaction. Anonymous feedback channels encourage honest responses.
  • Performance improvement plans: Structured workflows for managing underperforming employees with documented goals, timelines, and follow-up meetings.

BambooHR's performance management tools are deeply integrated with employee records — a review automatically attaches to the employee's profile, creating a historical record of performance over time. This is invaluable for promotion decisions, compensation reviews, and (unfortunately) termination documentation.

Gusto Performance Reviews (Plus/Premium Plans)

Gusto offers performance reviews, but they're more basic:

  • Review templates: Pre-built and customizable review forms for self-assessments and manager evaluations.
  • Review cycles: Schedule reviews on your preferred cadence.
  • Employee surveys: Basic employee feedback surveys.

Gusto's performance tools are functional for basic review cycles, but they lack the depth of BambooHR — no 360° feedback, no eNPS tracking, no goal tracking system, and no performance improvement plan workflows.

Performance Verdict

BambooHR wins by a wide margin. If performance management is important to your business — and it should be for any company past 15-20 employees — BambooHR's Pro plan offers capabilities that Gusto simply doesn't match. This alone is reason enough for some businesses to choose BambooHR, even if they prefer Gusto's payroll.

Benefits Administration

Gusto Benefits

Gusto is a licensed insurance broker in all 50 states — benefits aren't just administered, they're brokered:

  • Health, dental, and vision insurance from multiple carriers — Gusto shops across carriers to find the best rates for your business. No separate broker needed.
  • Life and disability insurance available as add-ons.
  • 401(k) retirement plans through Guideline partnership — easy setup, low fees, and integration with payroll for automatic contributions.
  • HSAs, FSAs, and commuter benefits — all administered through Gusto and integrated with payroll deductions.
  • 529 college savings plans — relatively unique offering for an SMB payroll platform.
  • Pay-as-you-go workers' compensation — premiums calculated from actual payroll data each pay period, eliminating large upfront deposits and year-end audits.
  • Benefits included in every plan — you don't pay extra for benefits administration on any Gusto tier.

Gusto's benefits experience is seamless because it's integrated with payroll. When an employee enrolls in health insurance, the payroll deduction is configured automatically. When a life event triggers a benefits change (marriage, new baby), the payroll deduction updates automatically. No manual reconciliation needed.

BambooHR Benefits

BambooHR's benefits are split between basic tracking (included) and full administration (add-on):

  • Benefits tracking (included in all plans): View and manage employee benefits enrollment data, plan details, and deduction records. Basic tracking of who is enrolled in what.
  • Benefits administration (paid add-on): Self-guided benefits configuration, open enrollment management, life event handling, new hire enrollment, election review and approval, carrier feed connections, and benefits reporting.

BambooHR is not an insurance broker — it doesn't shop for plans on your behalf or offer its own insurance marketplace. You bring your existing broker and carriers; BambooHR administers the enrollment and deductions. This is fine for businesses that already have a broker they like, but it's an extra step compared to Gusto's all-in-one approach.

Benefits Verdict

Gusto wins on benefits. The combination of built-in brokerage, automatic payroll integration, and no add-on fees makes Gusto the significantly easier and more affordable choice for benefits. BambooHR's benefits administration is functional, but it's an add-on cost, requires your own broker, and doesn't match the seamless payroll-benefits integration that Gusto offers out of the box.

Hiring & Onboarding

BambooHR Hiring

BambooHR has a full-featured applicant tracking system (ATS) built into the platform:

  • Job posting creation and distribution to job boards
  • Customizable application forms and candidate pipelines
  • Candidate communication templates and tracking
  • Interview scheduling and evaluation scorecards
  • Offer letter generation with e-signature
  • Seamless conversion from candidate to employee — all data transfers automatically to the employee record upon hire
  • Hiring analytics: time-to-hire, source tracking, pipeline metrics

BambooHR's onboarding is similarly comprehensive:

  • Pre-boarding task lists that start before day one (new hires complete paperwork at home)
  • Electronic document signing for offer letters, policies, and tax forms
  • IT provisioning checklists (assign equipment, accounts, access)
  • Custom onboarding workflows by role or department
  • Offboarding workflows with exit interviews and equipment return tracking

Gusto Hiring

Gusto's hiring tools are simpler but cover the essentials:

  • Job posting creation and distribution
  • Applicant tracking (Plus/Premium) — more basic than BambooHR's ATS
  • Offer letter templates with e-signature
  • I-9, W-4, and state tax form collection
  • E-Verify integration
  • Custom onboarding checklists with task assignments

Gusto's onboarding is focused on payroll and compliance readiness — making sure new hires are set up to be paid correctly from day one. BambooHR's onboarding is broader — it covers the full new-hire experience including IT setup, policy acknowledgment, and cultural integration.

Hiring & Onboarding Verdict

BambooHR wins on hiring and onboarding. Its ATS is more robust, the candidate-to-employee conversion is smoother, and the onboarding workflows are more comprehensive. Gusto covers the payroll-related basics (tax forms, direct deposit setup, compliance documents), but BambooHR handles the full employee lifecycle from first application to first day to last day.

Time Tracking & PTO

Gusto Time Tracking (Plus/Premium)

Gusto's time tracking is built into the Plus and Premium plans at no extra cost:

  • Mobile and desktop clock-in/out with GPS geolocation
  • Project tracking and workforce costing
  • Automatic overtime calculations based on state-specific rules
  • PTO accrual tracking with customizable policies
  • Manager approval workflows
  • Hours flow directly to payroll — no manual data entry or import

BambooHR Time Tracking (Paid Add-On)

BambooHR's time tracking is a separate paid add-on:

  • Mobile and desktop time clock
  • Project tracking and billable hours
  • Viewable and editable employee timesheets
  • Automated approval workflows
  • Automatic overtime calculations
  • Integration with BambooHR payroll add-on

BambooHR's PTO management is included in all plans (not just the time tracking add-on): time-off request and approval workflows, leave balance tracking, holiday calendars, and customizable PTO policies.

Time Tracking Verdict

Gusto wins on value — time tracking is included free on Plus/Premium plans, while BambooHR charges extra. Both offer similar capabilities (mobile clock-in, project tracking, overtime, approval workflows). The main difference is that BambooHR separates PTO management (included) from time tracking (paid add-on), which is confusing — you can track time off but not time worked without paying more.

  • Workforce planning visualizations and trend analysis

Gusto Reporting

Gusto's reporting is more focused on payroll and financial data:

  • Payroll journals and summaries
  • Tax liability and payment reports
  • Benefits enrollment and cost reports
  • Time tracking and PTO reports (Plus/Premium)
  • Workforce costing by project, department, or location (Plus/Premium)
  • Custom reports with basic filtering

Reporting Verdict

BambooHR wins on HR analytics; Gusto wins on payroll reporting. BambooHR's reporting is broader and deeper for people analytics — turnover trends, engagement data, performance patterns. Gusto's reporting is stronger for financial and payroll data — tax liabilities, labor costs, benefits expenses. Choose based on which data matters more to your business.

Ease of Use & Setup

Gusto

Gusto is consistently rated among the easiest payroll platforms to use. Key factors:

  • Self-service setup: No sales call required. Create an account, enter your business details, add employees, run payroll — all in 1-2 days.
  • No implementation fees. No consultants. No multi-week onboarding process.
  • Intuitive interface: Clean, modern design that non-technical users navigate easily. Payroll runs are guided step-by-step with clear summaries before approval.
  • Employee self-service: Employees can complete their own onboarding paperwork, view pay stubs, manage benefits, and update personal information without HR involvement.

BambooHR

BambooHR is also well-regarded for usability — it's one of the platform's highest-rated attributes:

  • Clean, intuitive interface: Top navigation bar provides easy access to Home, My Info, People, Hiring, Reports, and Payroll. Most functions are self-explanatory.
  • Setup requires a quote: You must contact sales for pricing, which adds friction. Implementation typically takes 6-12 weeks depending on company size and complexity.
  • Implementation fees: Expect 5-15% of annual software costs for setup assistance.
  • Employee self-service: Employees can update their profiles, request time off, view the directory, and access documents via web or mobile app.
  • 7-day free trial: Available but short — not enough time to fully evaluate the platform for complex needs.

Ease of Use Verdict

Gusto is faster to get started — no sales call, no implementation fees, setup in 1-2 days vs 6-12 weeks. Both platforms are well-designed and intuitive once you're up and running. BambooHR's longer setup is partly because it configures more systems (HR records, permissions, reporting, workflows) — it's a more comprehensive platform that takes more time to set up properly. For businesses that want to start paying people this week, Gusto wins. For businesses willing to invest 2-3 months in a proper HR system implementation, BambooHR's thorough setup pays off long-term.

Best Use Cases for Each

Choose Gusto When:

  • Payroll is your #1 priority — you need reliable, automatic payroll processing above all else
  • You're a small business (1-50 employees) that wants everything included at one price
  • You value transparent pricing — you want to know what you'll pay before you talk to anyone
  • You need to start fast — up and running in 1-2 days, not 6-12 weeks
  • Benefits brokerage matters — you want one platform to find, administer, and integrate health insurance with payroll
  • You pay contractors — especially international contractors in multiple countries
  • You run a restaurant, retail, or service business with hourly employees who benefit from features like Gusto Wallet and early wage access
  • Your HR needs are basic — employee records, PTO tracking, and basic compliance are sufficient

Choose BambooHR When:

  • HR management is your #1 priority — you need a comprehensive HRIS more than you need best-in-class payroll
  • You're a growing company (25-350 employees) that needs to professionalize HR operations
  • Performance management matters — you need goal tracking, 360° feedback, eNPS surveys, and structured review cycles
  • You have active recruiting needs — BambooHR's built-in ATS is stronger than Gusto's applicant tracking
  • Compliance and training tracking are important — especially in regulated industries (healthcare, finance, construction)
  • You want deeper people analytics — turnover trends, engagement data, workforce planning
  • You already have a payroll provider you like — BambooHR works with external payroll (including Gusto)
  • Company culture and employee experience are strategic priorities — BambooHR's engagement and community tools go deeper

Alternatives Worth Considering

  • ADP: The industry giant. Better for large, complex organizations with multi-state or multi-country operations. More expensive and complex than both Gusto and BambooHR, but offers the broadest range of payroll and HR services.
  • Rippling: The unified platform — HR, payroll, IT device management, and finance in one system. Best for tech companies and scaling businesses that want to replace 3-5 separate tools. More powerful than both Gusto and BambooHR, but more complex and expensive.
  • Deel: Best for international hiring. EOR services in 100+ countries, global payroll, and contractor management. Neither Gusto nor BambooHR competes on international hiring.
  • Paychex: Another established payroll provider. Stronger than Gusto for very complex payroll scenarios (union payroll, garnishments, multi-state) but less modern and transparent.
  • Paycom: Full-suite HR and payroll platform with its unique BETI (Better Employee Transaction Interface) tool. Strong for mid-market companies with 50-500+ employees.
  • Justworks: A Professional Employer Organization (PEO) that handles payroll, benefits, compliance, and HR through a co-employment model. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gusto or BambooHR better for small businesses?

It depends on your primary need. For payroll-focused businesses with 1-50 employees, Gusto is better — transparent pricing, everything included, faster setup. For HR-focused businesses with 25-350 employees that need performance management, recruiting, and deeper employee records, BambooHR offers more depth.

How much does Gusto cost compared to BambooHR in 2026?

Gusto Plus costs $80/month + $12/employee — so $380/month for 25 employees, all inclusive. BambooHR Pro with payroll, benefits, and time tracking add-ons typically runs $500-$700/month for 25 employees, plus implementation fees. Gusto is generally 30-40% cheaper for equivalent features.

Does BambooHR include payroll?

No — payroll is a paid add-on at $150/month for up to 20 employees, then ~$7.25/employee/month for additional employees. By contrast, every Gusto plan includes full-service payroll at no extra cost.

Which has better performance management?

BambooHR, by a significant margin. BambooHR Pro includes goal tracking, 360° feedback, eNPS surveys, and performance improvement plans. Gusto offers basic review templates on Plus/Premium plans but lacks the depth of BambooHR's performance suite.

Can I use BambooHR and Gusto together?

Yes — they have an official integration. You can use BambooHR as your HRIS (employee records, performance, hiring) and Gusto as your payroll engine. This gives you the best of both worlds, though you're paying for two platforms. Some businesses find this combination worthwhile.

Can either handle international employees?

Neither handles international full-time employees well. Gusto can pay international contractors in 80+ countries. BambooHR can store records for international employees but offers no international payroll or EOR services. For international hiring, consider Deel or Rippling instead.

Which is easier to set up?

Gusto — by a large margin. Self-service setup in 1-2 days with no implementation fees. BambooHR typically takes 6-12 weeks with a dedicated implementation process and fees of 5-15% of annual software costs.

Is Rippling better than both?

Rippling is more powerful than both — it combines HR, payroll, IT management, and finance into one unified platform. However, it's also more complex and expensive, starting at $8/employee/month for the base platform before adding modules. For businesses that need IT device management alongside HR, Rippling is worth considering. For businesses that just need payroll or just need an HRIS, Gusto or BambooHR respectively are simpler and more affordable choices.
