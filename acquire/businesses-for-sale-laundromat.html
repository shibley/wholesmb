Laundromats for Sale: Where to Find Listings and How to Evaluate Them

Ready to actually buy? This page covers where laundromats are listed for sale, what they typically cost, and how to size up a specific listing before you make an offer. Where laundromats are listed for sale

BizBuySell is the largest marketplace for laundromats for sale, with local listings searchable by city and state — it's the best place to start comparing real prices and revenue. Beyond it, business brokers who specialize in laundry and coin-operated businesses handle many deals and often know about listings before they go public. Equipment distributors, regional laundry associations, and direct owner outreach round out the off-market channel, where you'll face less competition but do more legwork.

Typical laundromat price ranges

Prices swing widely based on location, size, equipment age, and revenue. Use these ranges as a rough orientation, not gospel:

  • ~$50,000–$150,000 — smaller or older stores, secondary markets, or stores needing equipment upgrades.
  • ~$150,000–$500,000 — mid-size stores with steady revenue and reasonably modern machines.
  • $500,000–$1M+ — large, high-traffic, modern stores with new card-operated equipment and strong cash flow.

Most laundromats are priced as a multiple of annual cash flow (SDE), commonly around 2x–4x, adjusted for equipment condition and the remaining lease term.

How to evaluate a laundromat listing

A listing's headline numbers are a starting point, not the truth. Because laundromats are cash-heavy, the most important step is verifying revenue independently:

  • Water and utility bills — request 12+ months and compare them to the claimed revenue and machine turns. This is your best honesty check.
  • Equipment — note the age, brand, and water/energy efficiency of every washer and dryer; replacement is expensive.
  • Lease — confirm the remaining term, rent escalations, and whether it's assignable. A short lease is a major red flag.
  • Location & demand — check nearby competition and the share of renters in the area.
  • Add-on revenue — vending, ATM, and wash-dry-fold figures should be supported by records, not estimates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are laundromats listed for sale?

BizBuySell is the largest marketplace for laundromats for sale, searchable by city and state. Brokers who specialize in laundry and coin-operated businesses handle many deals, and some laundromats sell off-market through direct owner outreach or industry distributors.

What is the price range for a laundromat for sale?

They commonly list from about $50,000 to over $1 million. Smaller, older stores are at the low end; large, modern, high-traffic stores reach the top. Most are priced at roughly 2x–4x annual cash flow.

How do I evaluate a laundromat listing?

Verify the financials independently. Request 12+ months of water and utility bills and compare them to the claimed revenue, then check equipment age, the remaining lease term, local competition, and the share of nearby renters.

Should I buy a turnkey laundromat or a fixer-upper?

A turnkey store with newer machines and stable revenue costs more but carries less risk and produces cash immediately. A fixer-upper is cheaper with upside but needs capital, time, and experience. First-time buyers often prefer a proven, turnkey store.
