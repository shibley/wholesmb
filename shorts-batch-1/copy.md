## Video 2: The Seller Financing + SBA Loophole
**Hook / Script:**
"There is a legal loophole in SBA financing that lets you buy a business with the seller's money. 
Normally, the SBA requires a 10% equity injection from the buyer. 
But if the seller is willing to hold a 10% promissory note on 'full standby'—meaning they don't get paid for the life of the SBA loan—the bank counts that as your equity. 
You bring $0 to the closing table. 
You get a multi-million dollar business. 
The seller gets to retire and cash out 90% upfront."
