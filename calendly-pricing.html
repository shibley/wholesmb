Free Plan: What You Get (and Don't)

Calendly's Free plan is genuinely useful — not a bait-and-switch trial. Here's what's included:

✅ What's Included (Free):

  • Unlimited meetings — no cap on bookings per month
  • 1 active event type — e.g., "30-Minute Call" or "Discovery Session"
  • Calendar integrations — Google, Outlook, Office 365, iCloud (conflict checking)
  • Automated notifications — email confirmations and reminders to guests
  • Time zone detection — automatic conversion for global meetings
  • Basic customization — add your name, profile photo, simple description
  • Booking page link — shareable URL (e.g., calendly.com/yourname/30min)
  • Cancellation/rescheduling — guests can self-reschedule

❌ What's Missing (Free Limitations):

  • Only 1 event type at a time — if you offer 15min, 30min, 60min calls, you can only have ONE active
  • No SMS reminders — only email notifications (no-show rates are higher)
  • No custom branding — stuck with Calendly logo and colors on booking page
  • No integrations beyond calendar — no Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoom, Stripe, Zapier
  • No workflows or routing — can't automate follow-ups or route leads to specific team members
  • No analytics — can't track booking rates, no-shows, or team performance
  • No payment processing — can't charge for consultations or collect deposits

Who should use Free?

  • Freelancers who only need one type of meeting (e.g., "Client Call")
  • Side hustlers testing if scheduling automation is worth paying for
  • Personal use (e.g., scheduling coffee chats, 1-on-1s with friends)
  • Anyone booking fewer than 5 meetings per week

Who should upgrade? If you need multiple meeting types (15min, 30min, 60min), custom branding, SMS reminders, or CRM integrations, you'll hit Free's limits immediately. Upgrade to Essentials ($12/seat/mo).

Essentials Plan: $12/Seat/Month

Essentials (formerly called "Standard") is the entry-level paid plan. It's designed for small businesses, consultants, and anyone who needs more than 1 event type.

💵 Pricing:

  • $12 per seat/month (billed monthly)
  • $10 per seat/month (billed annually — save $24/year per user)
  • Example: 3-person team = $36/month or $30/month annual

✅ Everything in Free, plus:

  • Unlimited event types — create 15min, 30min, 60min, group calls, coffee chats, etc.
  • SMS reminders — reduce no-shows with text notifications (extra fees per SMS)
  • Custom branding — remove Calendly logo, add your colors/logo/favicon
  • Live chat support — email + chat (24/5 coverage)
  • Calendar invitations — send .ics files so meetings appear in guest calendars
  • Redirect after booking — send guests to thank-you page or custom URL
  • Custom confirmation page — personalized message after booking

❌ What's Still Missing:

  • No CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
  • No payment processing
  • No workflows or automation
  • No routing rules
  • No Zoom/Teams integrations
  • No team features (round-robin, pooled availability)

Who should use Essentials?

  • Coaches, consultants, therapists who need multiple session lengths
  • Small businesses (3-10 people) where each person manages their own calendar
  • Anyone who wants a professional booking page without Calendly branding
  • Teams that don't use a CRM or need advanced routing

Real-world example: A 5-person marketing agency pays $60/month ($12 × 5) for Essentials. Each team member has their own booking page with unlimited event types (30min Strategy Call, 60min Workshop, 15min Quick Check-In). They removed Calendly branding and added their agency logo. SMS reminders reduced no-shows from 15% to 5%.

Professional Plan: $16/Seat/Month

Professional is where Calendly gets powerful for sales teams, agencies, and anyone who needs CRM integrations or payment processing.

💵 Pricing:

  • $16 per seat/month (billed monthly)
  • $13 per seat/month (billed annually — save $36/year per user)
  • Example: 10-person sales team = $160/month or $130/month annual

✅ Everything in Essentials, plus:

  • Salesforce integration — auto-create leads, sync events, update contact records
  • HubSpot integration — 2-way sync with HubSpot CRM
  • Payment processing — collect payments via Stripe or PayPal at booking time
  • Workflows — trigger emails, webhooks, or Zapier actions after booking
  • Routing forms — qualify leads with questions, route to appropriate team member
  • 6-calendar conflict check — check availability across multiple calendars (work + personal)
  • Admin-managed event types — centrally manage templates for team
  • Zapier integration — connect to 5,000+ apps (Slack, Google Sheets, Airtable, etc.)
  • Remove "Powered by Calendly" footer — full white-label experience

❌ What's Still Missing:

  • No round-robin scheduling (can't distribute leads evenly across team)
  • No admin controls (can't manage user permissions)
  • No usage analytics (can't see team-wide booking rates)
  • No Zoom/Teams/GoToMeeting integrations (must add meeting links manually)
  • No SAML SSO or advanced security

Who should use Professional?

  • Sales teams who need Salesforce or HubSpot integration to log meetings automatically
  • Consultants and coaches who charge for sessions and need payment processing at booking
  • Agencies who use routing forms to qualify leads before booking discovery calls
  • Anyone using Zapier to connect Calendly to Slack, Airtable, Google Sheets, etc.

Real-world example: A 7-person SaaS sales team pays $112/month ($16 × 7) for Professional. Salesforce integration auto-creates leads when prospects book demos. Routing forms ask "Company size?" and "Budget?" to route enterprise leads to senior AEs and SMB leads to junior reps. Workflows send Slack notifications when VIP prospects book. Payment processing collects $500 deposits for implementation kickoff calls.

Payment processing fees: Stripe/PayPal charge 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. Calendly doesn't take a cut. Example: $100 consultation = you receive $97.10 ($100 - $2.90).

Teams Plan: $20/Seat/Month

Teams is designed for coordinated scheduling — when multiple people need to book meetings together (e.g., sales + solution engineer, recruiter + hiring manager).

💵 Pricing:

  • $20 per seat/month (billed monthly)
  • $16 per seat/month (billed annually — save $48/year per user)
  • Example: 15-person team = $300/month or $240/month annual

✅ Everything in Professional, plus:

  • Round-robin scheduling — distribute leads evenly across team (or by priority/expertise)
  • Pooled availability — show combined team calendar, book with first available person
  • Collective event types — schedule meetings with 2+ team members at once
  • Admin controls — manage user permissions, enforce branding, lock event types
  • Usage analytics — team-wide dashboards (booking rates, no-shows, top performers)
  • Zoom integration — auto-generate Zoom links for bookings (requires Zoom Pro license)
  • Microsoft Teams integration — auto-generate Teams meeting links
  • GoToMeeting integration
  • Salesforce Groups — advanced Salesforce routing and assignment rules
  • Priority support — faster response times, dedicated success manager for 20+ seats

Who should use Teams?

  • Sales organizations with multiple AEs who need round-robin lead distribution
  • Recruiting teams scheduling interviews with multiple interviewers
  • Customer success teams routing support calls across specialists
  • Agencies where client calls require 2+ team members (e.g., account manager + designer)
  • Admins who need to enforce branding, event type templates, and usage visibility

Real-world example: A 12-person sales team pays $240/month ($20 × 12) for Teams. Round-robin distributes inbound demo requests evenly across 4 AEs. For enterprise deals, collective event types auto-book 60min calls with AE + solutions engineer + CSM. Admin locks event type templates so reps can't change branding or remove qualification questions. Analytics dashboard shows: AE #1 has 80% show rate, AE #3 has 60% (needs coaching). Zoom integration auto-generates meeting links — no manual copying.

Zoom requirement: Zoom integration requires Zoom Pro license ($15.99/user/month) or higher. Factor this into total cost: $20 Calendly + $15.99 Zoom = $35.99/user/month.

Enterprise Plan: Custom Pricing

Enterprise is for large organizations (typically 50+ users) that need advanced security, compliance, and support.

💵 Pricing:

  • Custom — contact sales for quote
  • Typical range: $10,000-$50,000+ per year for 50-200 users
  • Includes implementation/onboarding fees (typically $3,000-$10,000)

✅ Everything in Teams, plus:

  • SAML-based single sign-on (SSO) — Okta, OneLogin, Azure AD integration
  • Advanced security controls — IP allowlisting, enforced 2FA, audit logs
  • Unlimited admin seats — no per-seat charge for admins/managers
  • Service Level Agreement (SLA) — guaranteed uptime, response times
  • Dedicated account manager — strategic support, quarterly business reviews
  • Custom integrations — API access, webhooks, custom Salesforce objects
  • Advanced reporting — custom dashboards, data exports, attribution tracking
  • Onboarding and training — dedicated implementation team, admin training
  • Priority feature requests — influence product roadmap

Who needs Enterprise?

  • Organizations with 50+ Calendly users
  • Companies requiring SAML SSO for compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA, etc.)
  • Enterprises with complex Salesforce setups requiring custom objects/routing
  • Organizations that need guaranteed uptime SLAs
  • Teams that require audit logs for security/compliance

How to get pricing: Contact Calendly sales (form on their website or call). Be ready to share: number of users, use case, required integrations, security requirements. Enterprise deals typically take 4-8 weeks to close (demo → security review → procurement → contract).

Hidden Costs & Extra Fees

Calendly's pricing is mostly transparent, but a few extra costs can surprise you:

1. SMS Reminder Fees

SMS reminders (available on Essentials+) reduce no-shows by 30-50%, but they cost extra:

  • US/Canada: ~$0.01-0.02 per SMS
  • UK/Europe: ~$0.03-0.05 per SMS
  • Other countries: $0.05-0.15 per SMS

Example: 100 bookings/month with 2 SMS reminders each (48 hours before + 2 hours before) = 200 SMS × $0.02 = $4/month extra. Not huge, but adds up for high-volume teams.

2. Payment Processing Fees

If you use Calendly's payment processing (Professional+ only):

  • Stripe: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
  • PayPal: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (US), slightly higher internationally

Calendly doesn't take a cut, but Stripe/PayPal do. Example: $200 consultation = you receive $194.40 ($200 - $5.60).

3. Zoom Pro License Requirement

Calendly's Zoom integration (Teams+ only) requires each user to have a Zoom Pro license or higher:

  • Zoom Pro: $15.99/user/month
  • Zoom Business: $21.99/user/month (10+ licenses)

If you're paying $20/month for Calendly Teams + $15.99 for Zoom Pro = $35.99/user/month total. Factor this into budgeting.

4. Annual Prepayment (Not a Hidden Cost, But Worth Noting)

Annual billing saves 20%, but you pay upfront:

  • Essentials: $120/year per seat (vs $144 monthly billing)
  • Professional: $156/year per seat (vs $192 monthly)
  • Teams: $192/year per seat (vs $240 monthly)

For a 10-person team on Professional, annual billing is $1,560 upfront (vs $1,920 over 12 months) — saves $360. Only worth it if you're confident you won't churn.

5. Enterprise Implementation Fees

Enterprise plans include onboarding/implementation fees:

  • Typical range: $3,000-$10,000 (one-time)
  • Covers: dedicated onboarding, custom Salesforce setup, admin training, API configuration

This is negotiable — ask for it to be waived or reduced if you're committing to a multi-year contract.

What's NOT charged extra:

  • ✅ No per-meeting fees
  • ✅ No per-booking fees
  • ✅ No setup fees (except Enterprise)
  • ✅ No overage fees (unlimited meetings on all plans)
  • ✅ No charge for calendar integrations (Google, Outlook, iCloud)
  • ✅ No charge for webhook/API usage (Professional+)

Should You Upgrade? Decision Framework

Here's how to decide which plan is right for you:

Stay on Free if:

  • You only offer ONE type of meeting (e.g., always "30-Minute Call")
  • You don't care about branding (Calendly logo is fine)
  • You book fewer than 5 meetings per week
  • You're testing if scheduling automation is worth it

Upgrade to Essentials ($12/seat) if:

  • You need multiple event types (15min, 30min, 60min, group calls, etc.)
  • You want professional branding (remove Calendly logo, add your colors/logo)
  • You want SMS reminders to reduce no-shows
  • You're booking 10+ meetings per week (time savings justify $12/month)

Upgrade to Professional ($16/seat) if:

  • You use Salesforce or HubSpot and want auto-logging of meetings
  • You want to collect payments at booking time (coaching, consulting, deposits)
  • You need routing forms to qualify leads before booking
  • You use Zapier to connect Calendly to other tools (Slack, Airtable, Google Sheets)
  • You want to check availability across 6+ calendars (work + personal + team calendars)

Upgrade to Teams ($20/seat) if:

  • You need round-robin scheduling to distribute leads evenly across your team
  • You schedule meetings with 2+ team members (collective events)
  • You need admin controls to enforce branding/templates/permissions
  • You want usage analytics to track booking rates, no-shows, and team performance
  • You use Zoom/Teams/GoToMeeting and want auto-generated meeting links

Upgrade to Enterprise if:

  • You have 50+ users (volume discounts kick in)
  • You require SAML SSO for security/compliance
  • You need guaranteed uptime SLAs
  • You have complex Salesforce routing or custom integrations

Time-savings calculation:

Calendly saves ~5-10 minutes per meeting booked (vs back-and-forth email). If you book 20 meetings/month:

  • 20 meetings × 7 minutes saved = 140 minutes/month (2.3 hours)
  • At $50/hour value → Calendly saves $115/month in time
  • Essentials at $12/month → 9.6× ROI

For sales teams: if Calendly increases show rates from 70% to 85% (via SMS reminders + easier rescheduling), that's 3 extra meetings per 20 bookings. If each meeting is worth $500 in pipeline → $1,500/month value from $16/seat investment.

Can You Negotiate Calendly Pricing?

Standard plans (Free, Essentials, Professional, Teams) have fixed pricing — no negotiation. But you CAN negotiate:

1. Annual Prepayment Discount (Everyone)

All paid plans offer 20% off for annual billing. This is automatic — no negotiation needed. Just select "Annual" at checkout.

2. Enterprise Volume Discounts (50+ Users)

Once you hit 50+ users, you qualify for Enterprise pricing with volume discounts:

  • 50-100 users: 10-15% discount
  • 100-500 users: 15-25% discount
  • 500+ users: 25-35% discount

Negotiation tips: get competitive quotes from Chili Piper, Cal.com Enterprise, or Microsoft Bookings to use as leverage.

3. Multi-Year Contracts (Enterprise Only)

Committing to 2-3 years can get you:

  • 10-20% additional discount on top of volume pricing
  • Waived implementation fees ($3K-10K)
  • Locked-in pricing (no price increases during contract term)

4. Nonprofit/Education Discounts

Calendly offers discounts for nonprofits and educational institutions:

  • Nonprofits (501(c)(3)): 20-30% discount on Professional/Teams plans
  • Educational institutions: Custom pricing (contact sales)

Apply via Calendly's nonprofit/education request form.

5. Startup Programs

If you're part of Y Combinator, Techstars, or similar accelerators, ask your network for referral credits. Some accelerators have partnerships with Calendly for free/discounted plans (typically 6-12 months free on Professional or Teams).

What you CAN'T negotiate:

  • ❌ Per-seat pricing on Essentials/Professional/Teams (fixed at $12/$16/$20)
  • ❌ Free plan features (it's a marketing tool, not negotiable)
  • ❌ Payment processing fees (Stripe/PayPal set these, not Calendly)
  • ❌ SMS fees (set by telecom providers, not Calendly)

Free Trial Details

Calendly's trial setup is a bit confusing — here's how it actually works:

Free Plan = Forever Free (Not a Trial)

The Free plan is genuinely free forever — not a 14-day trial. You can use it indefinitely with 1 event type, unlimited meetings, calendar sync, and basic features. No credit card required.

Paid Plan Trials (14 Days)

If you sign up for Essentials, Professional, or Teams, you get a 14-day free trial:

  • Full access to all features during trial
  • Credit card required at signup
  • Auto-converts to paid after 14 days unless you cancel
  • You can downgrade to Free before trial ends (keeps your account, loses paid features)

How to maximize the trial:

  1. Day 1: Connect your calendar (Google/Outlook), create 3-5 event types, customize branding
  2. Day 2-5: Set up integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier) and test workflows
  3. Day 6-10: Book real meetings. Measure: Does it save time? Do guests like the UX? Does CRM integration work?
  4. Day 11-13: Check analytics (Teams plan only). Decide if paid features justify cost.
  5. Day 14: Decide: upgrade, downgrade to Free, or cancel.

How to cancel/downgrade:

  • Settings → Billing → "Change plan" → select Free or Cancel
  • If you downgrade to Free, your account stays active (you keep your booking history)
  • If you cancel, your account is deleted after 30 days (export data first)

Enterprise trials: Enterprise plans don't have self-service trials. You go through a sales process (demo → pilot → contract). Pilots are typically 30-60 days with 10-20 pilot users before rolling out to full org.

FAQs

How much does Calendly cost per month?

Calendly pricing ranges from $0 (Free) to $12/seat/month (Essentials), $16/seat/month (Professional), $20/seat/month (Teams), and custom pricing (Enterprise). Annual billing saves 20%. For a 5-person team: Essentials costs $60/month, Professional $80/month, Teams $100/month. Most small businesses start with Essentials or Professional.

Is there a free version of Calendly?

Yes, Calendly has a forever-free plan that includes 1 event type, unlimited meetings, calendar integrations (Google, Outlook, iCloud), automated notifications, and basic customization. It's great for solopreneurs and freelancers. Limitations: only 1 active event type, no reminders, no workflows, Calendly branding on booking pages. Upgrade to Essentials ($12/seat/mo) for unlimited event types, SMS reminders, and custom branding.

What's the difference between Calendly Essentials, Professional, and Teams?

Essentials ($12/seat/mo) adds unlimited event types, reminders, custom branding, and team scheduling. Professional ($16/seat/mo) adds Salesforce integration, payment processing (Stripe/PayPal), workflows, and advanced routing. Teams ($20/seat/mo) adds round-robin scheduling, admin controls, usage analytics, Zoom integrations, and priority support. Choose Essentials for basic scheduling, Professional for sales teams, Teams for coordinated team booking.

Does Calendly charge per user or per account?

Calendly charges per seat (per user) on paid plans. Each team member who needs their own scheduling page requires a paid seat. The Free plan is single-user only. On Essentials, Professional, or Teams, you pay per active user. Example: 3-person sales team on Professional = $48/month ($16 × 3). You can add or remove seats anytime, and billing is prorated.

Are there hidden fees with Calendly?

Calendly has minimal hidden fees. Main costs: payment processing fees (Stripe/PayPal 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction on Professional+), SMS reminder fees (varies by country, typically $0.01-0.05 per SMS), and Zoom Pro license required for Zoom integrations ($15.99/mo per user). Annual prepayment gets 20% discount. Enterprise pricing includes implementation fees (typically $3,000-10,000 for 50+ users). No per-meeting or per-booking fees.

Can I cancel Calendly anytime?

Yes, monthly plans can be canceled anytime with no penalty. Your subscription remains active through the end of your billing period. Annual plans can be canceled but don't offer prorated refunds — you keep access until the annual term ends. Downgrading from paid to Free is instant, but you'll lose paid features (unlimited event types, reminders, branding, integrations) immediately. Export your data before downgrading.

Is Calendly worth it for a small business?

Calendly is worth it if you book 10+ meetings per week and value time savings over cost. At $12-16/seat/month, it pays for itself if it saves 30 minutes of scheduling back-and-forth per week. Worth it for: sales teams, customer success, consultants, recruiters, agencies. Not worth it if: you only book 2-3 meetings per week (use Free plan), you need advanced CRM workflows (try Chili Piper), you're on a tight budget (try Calendly Free or Cal.com open-source alternative).

Does Calendly integrate with my calendar and CRM?

Calendly integrates with Google Calendar, Outlook, Office 365, and iCloud (all plans including Free). CRM integrations require Professional or Teams: Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot. Teams plan adds Zoom, Microsoft Teams, GoToMeeting. Enterprise adds custom integrations via API and webhooks. Zapier integration (Professional+) connects to 5,000+ apps. Check calendar conflicts across 6 calendars on Professional+.

✅ Worth it if:

  • You book 10+ meetings per week and scheduling back-and-forth wastes hours of your time

❌ Not worth it if:

  • You only book 2-5 meetings per week — stick with Calendly Free or use email
  • You're extremely budget-conscious — try Cal.com (open-source) or Microsoft Bookings (free with M365)
  • You need advanced sales automation beyond basic routing — Chili Piper has better lead qualification and routing (but costs 2x)
  • You run a service business (salon, gym, clinic) with class bookings and package management — Acuity Scheduling is better suited

Best plan for most small businesses:

  • Free: Solopreneurs booking <10 meetings/month who only need one event type
  • Essentials ($12/seat): 3-10 person teams who need branding + multiple event types but no CRM integration
  • Professional ($16/seat): Sales teams, consultants, or anyone using Salesforce/HubSpot — CRM integration alone justifies the $4/month upgrade
  • Teams ($20/seat): 10-50 person teams that need round-robin, collective events, or Zoom integration

Bottom line: Calendly is the market leader for a reason — it's polished, reliable, and integrates with everything. If scheduling is a meaningful part of your workflow, it's worth paying for Professional or Teams. If you're just getting started or booking rarely, the Free plan is surprisingly capable.

Start with the Free plan, upgrade to Essentials when you hit its limits, then move to Professional when you add a CRM. That's the natural progression for most businesses.
